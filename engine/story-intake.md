# Story intake (how a story the founder tells becomes an episode)

The founder, Nov 11: "if there is a way where i can just drop my stories in some chat can be the same on or
the thread or porject and it can turn it into episodes where pxi is involved it would be nice. just as plot
and things inspo not a 1 to 1 story copying."

## For the founder

Tell the story in the project chat or any thread, as roughly as you like. Voice-note style is fine. Start it
with "story:" if you want to be sure it is caught. Nothing else is needed.

## For whoever receives it (the coordinator, a thread, or a scheduled run)

Do this in the same turn. It is small, and it needs no stage card.

1. **Bank it.** Append it to `engine/real-stories.md` as the next `R<n>`, in the founder's words, with the
   date. Replace real names, including the founder's own, with a role ("a friend", "[the founder]"). Leave
   out anything the founder says is not for an episode.
2. **Say what is in it**, in the same form as the stories already there: the engine (`bible/story.md`, section
   3, or a machine from `bible/gumball-lens.md`), the best beat, the calm room, the feeling, the button, and
   where PXI fits or NONE.
3. **Write one or two premises** into `engine/premise-bank.md` as CANDIDATE, tagged `From: R<n>`, with the
   tests in `bible/story.md` section 5 answered. Change the names, the place and at least two details. One
   story can feed several episodes: a whole plot, a single beat, a character who returns.
4. **Tell the founder in one or two lines** what it became, in plain words, and ask nothing unless a detail is
   needed to make it work.
5. Commit and push with the state, like any other change.

## Rules

- Inspiration, never a transcript. Nobody real is recognisable.
- The story decides whether PXI is in it (`bible/story.md`, section 4). Never bend a good story to fit a feature.
- A story about a friend is told with the fondness the founder told it with.
- A story is never used up. Mark `USED BY: <episode id>` next to it and leave it in the bank.
- If the founder says a thing is not for the show, it is not banked.
