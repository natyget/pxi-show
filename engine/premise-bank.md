# Premise bank

One premise each. A premise gets in only if the nine tests in `bible/story.md` (section 5) have a one-line
answer and nothing in it is on the Never list in `bible/humor.md`. Stage 0, stage 1 and the story intake
(`engine/story-intake.md`) add here. Stage 1 takes the top APPROVED one. Only the founder marks a premise
APPROVED.

Format:

```
- [CANDIDATE] **Title.** <one ordinary thing: Nio wants X, but Y happens, and he responds by Z>
  Ego: whose · Kind: from `bible/gumball-style.md` section 2 · PXI: ENGINE | MOMENT | NONE, and what ·
  From: R<n>, the slate in `bible/world.md`, or original
```
