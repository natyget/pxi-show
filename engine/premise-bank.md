# Premise bank

One premise each. A premise gets in only if all nine tests in `bible/story.md` (section 3) have a one-line
answer, and it passes the three tests in `bible/humor.md`. Stage 0 and stage 1 add here. Stage 1 takes the top
APPROVED one. Only the founder marks a premise APPROVED.

Format:

```
- [CANDIDATE] <the retell: one sentence a viewer would say to a friend>
  Problem: … · Want: … · Stakes and clock: … · PXI answer: … · Turn: … · Point: … · Send: …
```

## Premises

- [CANDIDATE] Nio has lived here three months and is not in a single photo, and his mum wants one of him with
  friends by Sunday.
  Problem: nobody takes pictures of the new guy · Want: one photo of him with people, by Sunday · Stakes and
  clock: his mum calls Sunday, and he has told her he has friends · PXI answer: he goes to things and lets
  everyone's phones do it, then waits for "You might be in a photo" · Turn: face match finds him in the
  background of a dozen strangers' photos, always alone, always near the same person · Point: he was less alone
  than he thought; the person is Maya · Send: to the friend who is never in the photos.
- [CANDIDATE] Nio knows nobody, so he puts his own birthday on the map and invites the whole city.
  Problem: a birthday with no one to ask · Want: ten people in the room by nine · Stakes and clock: the venue
  needs ten or it gives the room away at nine · PXI answer: he makes the event and it shows on everyone's map ·
  Turn: the people who come are all there for the same reason he is · Point: the room fills with people who had
  nowhere to be; that is his first circle · Send: to the friend with a birthday coming and no plan.
- [CANDIDATE] Nio keeps finding out about the best night of the week from other people's photos, so tonight he
  goes to everything on the map.
  Problem: always hearing about it after · Want: to be at the thing everyone talks about tomorrow · Stakes and
  clock: one night, and Maya has bet him he will be home by ten · PXI answer: the Discover map, every pin ·
  Turn: the thing everyone talks about tomorrow is the one he left early · Point: you cannot be everywhere, and
  the album has him in it anyway · Send: to the friend who always leaves before it gets good.
- [CANDIDATE] Nio cannot fly home for Thanksgiving, so he puts a dinner on the map for everyone else who can't.
  Problem: a holiday alone in a new city · Want: a full table · Stakes and clock: the turkey is for twelve and
  it is already in the oven · PXI answer: the event, the invite link, and the thread where strangers decide who
  brings what · Turn: everyone brings the same thing · Point: a table of strangers with nine pies is still a
  table, and the album is the proof he sends home · Send: to anyone not going home this year.
