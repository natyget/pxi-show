# Holiday calendar

PLAN reads this every week. A holiday episode models how to use PXI on that day, so that people who watch it do
the same. The PXI behaviour comes only from features in `bible/pxi-product.md`.

**Dates for every holiday release**, counted back from the release day:
script approved by day −14 · proof passed by day −9 · build passed by day −5 · founder approval by day −3 ·
scheduled by day −2. If a date is missed, the holiday drops to a FOURTH WALL, FILLER or STILL from the menu, and
PLAN says so in the report. An episode is never rushed and never released after its day.

Release on the day, or the evening before when the day itself is spent offline.

Holidays that move or that the house has not checked (Super Bowl Sunday, Lunar New Year, Easter, Eid, Diwali,
Mother's Day, Father's Day, Hanukkah) are added by PLAN with a date it has looked up, one quarter ahead.

**Thanksgiving 2026:** the pilot is not approved yet, so the dates above cannot be met for a full episode unless
the pilot is approved by about 16 Nov. If it is not, Thanksgiving gets a FOURTH WALL or a STILL, and the first
full holiday episode is New Year's Eve.

Birthdays, local events and trends are not on this calendar. PLAN may propose one when the playbook says it is
worth an episode slot.
