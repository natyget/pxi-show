# Captions and search (how a release gets found)

The founder, Nov 12: "i learned people look up Whats the appo that you can take photos on and share instantly
into an album and find my profile so captions should be key too. so in the scheduler lets back the find events
near you and all the good seo things we used".

People find PXI by typing a need, not a name. So every caption does two jobs: it is funny, and it says, in the
words a person would type, what PXI is for. PACKAGE and DAILY write captions from this page. SCHEDULE posts them
exactly as written.

## The two needs people type

| The need | Words people use | PXI's answer, said plainly |
|---|---|---|
| **Photos from the night** | "what's the app that you can take photos on and share instantly into an album", shared photo album app, app where everyone's photos go in one album, friend group photo ideas | everyone's photos from the night land in one shared album, and you get every photo you're in |
| **Something to do** | find events near you, events near me, things to do tonight, things to do this weekend, what's on near me, night out ideas | every event near you on one map |

The website already answers these searches (the event discovery and shared photo gallery pages on
pxispace.com), so a caption that uses the same plain words sends people somewhere that matches.

The founder's note also says people look up the profile. So the handle, the name on the profile and the bio
should say PXI and both needs. That is a change to the accounts, so it is proposed to the founder, never made
by a run.

## The shape of every caption

1. **The joke, first.** One plain line a funny 22-year-old would post. It comes from the script's `## Caption`.
   It starts from what the video means, and never retypes a line or an on-screen word.
2. **The search line.** One sentence, in a person's voice, that names PXI, says it is a free app on the App
   Store and Google Play, and states one of the two needs in the words from the table. Pick the need the
   episode showed. If PXI was not in the picture, use "something to do": the map is the simplest sell.
3. Nothing else, unless the platform row below adds it.

Example: `he carried him to the car and the car was already full 😭 (pxi is a free app on the app store and
google play. everyone's photos from the night land in one shared album, so you get every photo you're in)`

The search line changes every time in how it arrives (a parenthesis, a P.S., a confession), and it always
keeps the plain words. It is one sentence. A caption stuffed with phrases reads as an advert and fails the
friend test in `bible/craft-library.md`.

## By platform

| Platform | Caption | Also fill in |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram, Facebook | The joke, then the search line. No hashtags | Alt text: one plain sentence saying what is in the video, with the need in it |
| TikTok | The joke, then a shorter search line | 3 or 4 hashtags: `#thingstodo` or `#photoapp` by need, `#pxi`, one for the topic, and one city tag only when the episode is plainly set there. The words Nio says in the tag count as search text too, so the tag says the need out loud |
| YouTube Shorts | Title: the hook in under 60 characters, then `#shorts` | Description: the joke, the search line, and the link. Tags: things to do, events near me, shared photo album app, pxi. Not made for kids |
| YouTube long cut | Title: what happens, as a person would search or retell it, under 70 characters. Never an episode number | Description: two sentences on the episode, the search line, the link, and both needs once each in plain sentences |

Links carry where they came from: `pxispace.com/bio?utm_source=<platform>`.

## Rules carried over from the founder's earlier caption notes

- No engagement bait: no "comment below", "tag a friend", "which one are you".
- No @ tags. No city names in a caption; a city only ever appears as a TikTok hashtag.
- One or two emoji, only where a person would put one. Lower case is fine.
- Never compare fees. Never "the morning after".
- Do not repeat a caption idea used in the last 30 days (`engine/learning.md` ledger).
- A question for the comments about the story's lore is unconfirmed (it came from the pasted brief and sits
  close to engagement bait). Leave it out until the founder says.

## Real events

When a story or a daily post points at something on the map, prefer an event that is really on PXI that week
(`bible/world.md`, Real events). PLAN may look at the public events feed on pxispace.com to find one. It is
never required and nothing is bent to fit a listing. A real event is named in a caption only with the
founder's approval of that post.

## What is tested

These rules are starting assumptions from the old social engine and the founder's note, not results. LEARN
compares the two needs as search lines, and keeps what brings profile visits, link clicks and follows
(`engine/learning.md`, experiment E5).
