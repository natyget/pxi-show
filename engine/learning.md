# Learning loop

LEARN runs once a week. It reads this file and `engine/playbook.md`, pulls numbers for @pxi.app from Metricool
and Supermetrics, updates the ledger below and rewrites the playbook. It reads no scripts and opens no video.

## What gets recorded for every release (one row in the ledger)

date · platform · format · episode id · cut (short or long) · length in seconds · hook type · look · call to
action type · holiday · trial reel or not · experiment id and arm

## What gets measured

The founder, Nov 12: "we want more people to follow us not like and comment but follow". The score of a release
is **follows per 1,000 people reached**. Everything else explains the score. Views alone decide nothing, and
likes and comments decide nothing.

1. **Follows per 1,000 reached** (the score). Instagram per reel from Supermetrics `media_follows`; TikTok from
   the account's gain in the two days after posting, marked "acct".
2. **Full watches:** completion (TikTok full-watch rate, Instagram average watch time over length). The people
   who finish are the warm audience in `engine/promotion.md`.
3. **Hold:** the share still watching after three seconds.
4. **Sends for each person reached.** Sends matter most with people who do not follow yet.
5. **Profile visits, bio link clicks and app store clicks** (the road from follower to app user).
6. Saves, likes and comments, as context only.

For a trial reel: is watch time above the account's average, are strangers sending it, and are they following.

## How a test is run

- One variable at a time: hook, short-cut length, cover, caption, call to action, posting time, or format.
- Each arm needs at least three releases before anything is concluded. Until then the ledger says "open".
- A result is a sentence with numbers in it. If the difference is small, the result is "no difference".
- The mix: about 70% of releases use what is winning, 20% are variations of it, 10% are something new.
- A different look is tested only in DREAM and FILLER releases. If one wins three times, propose it to the
  founder. Do not change the locked style.
- Never tested, never changed by this loop: the characters, the voices, the humor doctrine, the rule that PXI
  is real and the story is clean, and the approval gate.

## What LEARN writes

- New rows in the ledger, and the state of each open experiment.
- `engine/playbook.md`, rewritten in full, 40 lines at most.
- At most one proposal for the founder, in the report, with the numbers that support it.

## Promotion (paid)

The ladder, the winner rules, the warm audience of 95% watchers and the budgets are in `engine/promotion.md`.
In short: boost organic winners for follows, collect the people who watch to the end and engage, and show them
the Nio app videos that already won. Judge on cost per follow and cost per install, never views. Every campaign
is built paused and switched on only on the founder's recorded yes.

## Comments and search

- Each week, read the comments on the releases in the ledger. Reply as the brand, short and human, and flag
  anything sensitive for the founder.
- Collect what people keep asking or saying in their own words: what the app is, whether it is in their city,
  how the map or the photo matching works, what they searched for. Put the recurring ones in
  `engine/audience-notes.md` with a count.
- Once a month, turn those into at most five proposals for the website (pages, headings, questions to answer,
  the words people actually use) and hand them to the website's own thread. The show's runs do not edit the
  website.
- The same notes feed the premise bank: a problem viewers keep describing is a premise.
