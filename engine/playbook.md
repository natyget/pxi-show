# Playbook (what is working now)

Rewritten in full by LEARN every week. 40 lines at most. WRITE and PACKAGE read it. Nothing here overrides the
bible or the style lock.

**Status: no releases yet. Everything below is a starting assumption, not a result.**

## Hooks
- Open on motion and sound in the first second: a face reacting, a phone lighting up, or the middle of an action.
- No titles, greetings or anything that looks like an ad at the start.

## Cuts
- Short cut 30 to 50 seconds. Long cut up to 3 minutes. Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts all take at least 3 minutes.

## Calls to action
- Nio's tag after the button, different every time. Default subject: the map of everything on near you.

## Captions and covers
- The joke first, in one plain line. Then one line saying PXI is a free app and what it does.
- The cover is a face in a take, never a title card.

## Posting
- Instagram first as a trial reel. Move it to the grid and the other platforms when it beats the account's
  average watch time and strangers are sending it.

## Platform rules checked
- AI disclosure: not checked yet. Check at the first upload on each platform and record the date here.
