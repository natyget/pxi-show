# Format menu

Every release is one of these. PLAN picks the mix, LEARN adjusts it. Every format carries a PXI call to action
in the slot named here, and every format goes through the accident check before the founder sees it.

| Format | What it is | Length | Uses | Call to action | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EPISODE | One story on one PXI feature. Built as a long cut with a short cut marked inside it | Long cut up to 3 min; short cut 30 to 50 s | The full line, stages 1 to 6 | Tag at the end, plus caption | High |
| HOLIDAY | An episode with a date, modelling how to use PXI that day (`engine/calendar.md`) | Same as an episode | The full line, with the calendar's dates | Tag that asks the viewer to do the same today | High |
| FOURTH WALL | Nio talks to the viewer, flat and businesslike, like a man explaining a plan. The discovery map is the default subject | 10 to 25 s | Existing set, one or two shots, one phone insert | The piece is the ask, kept fun and cute | Low |
| DREAM | Uncanny on purpose. No plot or PXI needed in the picture. A different look is allowed and declared in the ledger | 10 to 30 s | Existing rigs; one new idea | Caption only | Medium |
| FILLER | One gag in one place. A take, a background character, a moment between episodes | 6 to 20 s | Existing sets and rigs only | End frame or caption | Low |
| LOOP | A seamless loop or GIF cut from a take that already exists | 2 to 6 s | No new animation | Caption | Very low |
| STILL | A frame, a phone screen from the world, or a carousel (for example the album "nio being nio") | Image | No new animation | Caption, and the last slide of a carousel | Very low |

## Rules for every format

- **Whole, never chopped.** No "Part 1". A short cut is a complete joke with its own opening, not the first
  minute of the long cut. Say "stay tuned" only when a story really ends on a cliffhanger.
- **No intro, no outro, no logo sting** unless the piece calls for one. It starts on motion and sound.
- **The first second** is a picture that asks a question: something moving, a face reacting, a phone lighting up.
  Never a title, never a greeting, never anything that looks like an ad.
- **It ends on the joke.** The tag comes after the button and is short. It never asks for likes or follows in a
  generic way. Asking viewers to follow for the next episode is allowed when there is a next episode.
- **The call to action is Nio's**, in his voice, and it changes every time. The standing idea: get PXI so you can
  see what is on near you and come to his things. The current best versions are in `engine/playbook.md`.
- **Sound off works.** The story reads from the picture and the subtitles.

## The cuts of an episode

- The script marks the short-cut path: the shots that make a complete 30 to 50 second version.
- BUILD makes the short cut first. It can ship without the long cut if the long cut is late.
- Short cut: Instagram Reels (as a trial reel first), TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook.
- Long cut: YouTube, and other platforms when the short cut has already won.
- Each platform gets its own caption and cover. Nothing is cross-posted with another app's watermark.

## A week in production (starting point, LEARN changes it)

One EPISODE or HOLIDAY, one FOURTH WALL, one FILLER or DREAM, and two or three LOOP or STILL posts cut from the
episode. The cheap formats come from material the episode already made.
