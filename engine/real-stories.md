# Real stories (the founder's, banked as inspiration)

Things that really happened, in the founder's words, each with what the room should take from it. They are
**inspiration and never a transcript**: "just as plot and things inspo not a 1 to 1 story copying" (founder,
Nov 11). `engine/story-intake.md` says how a story gets here and how it becomes a premise.

Rules for using any of them:

- Change every name, place and identifying detail. Nobody real can be recognised, and no real company is named
  (a rideshare is "a ride", not a brand).
- Keep what was funny, what was strange, and what it felt like. Drop anything that only makes sense if you
  were there.
- Nio is the founder's stand-in, so Nio does what the founder did.
- A person in a story is never the joke for being ill, foreign, drunk or in love. The joke is what they do
  and how calmly everyone takes it.
- The standard the founder set: "episodes of this calibar and better are what i expect."
