# Stage P: PLAN (weekly)

**Model:** the run itself (Sonnet). No subagents.

**Read:** `bible/gumball-style.md` (section 2), `engine/calendar.md`, `engine/formats.md`, `engine/playbook.md`, `engine/premise-bank.md`,
`bible/callbacks.md`, and the last eight lines of `bible/timeline.md`.

**Do:**
1. List every holiday in the next six weeks with its five dates. For each, say whether it gets an episode or a
   cheaper format, and why. If a holiday's date is not in the calendar, look it up and add it.
2. Write the next two weeks of releases into the Queue in `STATE.md`: date, format, episode id or source
   episode, tone, plot engine, and the PXI behaviour it shows or NONE. Follow the week shape in `formats.md`
   and the mix in the playbook. Check the rotation: never the same kind of story two episodes running, with the spread in
   `bible/gumball-style.md` section 2 as the aim; mostly funny, with no three sad in a row; at most one episode in four with no PXI in the picture; Maya in about four episodes of ten; and every
   third episode marked to pay off a plant at least five episodes old (`bible/callbacks.md`).
3. Check the bank. If fewer than three premises are APPROVED, list the five best candidates in the report and
   ask the founder to pick.
4. Set `stage` and `stage_card` to the next stage of the story that is furthest behind its date.

**Gate:** every holiday in range has a format and dates, and the queue covers two weeks.
**Report:** the two weeks in one line each, and anything the founder has to pick.
