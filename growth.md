# What to post and how we learn

Goal: 300 followers on @pxi.app in about 8 weeks, and a viewer hooked enough that the second video they see makes
them download the app.

## Three phases

| Phase | Weeks | Target | What happens | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Find the voice | 1 to 2 | 50 | City keyword in the profile name. 5 videos and 2 carousels a week. Try 5 hook types once each. Trial-reel every main reel | One post at 2x the average views |
| Repeat what works | 3 to 5 | 150 | Keep the top 2, drop the bottom 2. The Thursday weekend map. Ask 3 organizers for Collabs. Test one ending a week | 150 followers, else repeat a week |
| Borrow audiences | 6 to 8 | 300 | Collab posts every week. Winners again as photo posts. Double down on the best hook | |

## Formats (rotate; never the same one twice in a row)

| Format | What it is | Size | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| City discovery reel | NYC or Boston, this weekend: real listings with their real posters, as the app's own Discover cards (`studio/events.py`, `studio/card.py`) | 30 to 45 s | Reach. The lead format |
| Feature story | One feature inside a small story with one joke. `videos/2026-11-24-idk-to-omw` is the model | 30 to 45 s | Do people get what PXI does |
| Nio to camera | Nio, the guy who built PXI, talks to the viewer in his own voice: why he built it, what he is fixing. The one format where he looks into the lens. No download or follower numbers unless the founder asks for a milestone post | 20 to 40 s | Does founder-style content earn follows |
| The mess and the scrapbook | Nio throws or goes to an event and it goes wrong on screen. The end is the scrapbook: the same night the way the photos remember it | 45 to 90 s | Sends. The album and the camera |
| Follow-along | A funny situation with no pitch. Someone uses PXI the way a person would tonight | 20 to 45 s | Watch time |
| The invite | Nio is busy with something dumb. A PXI invite lands (a sleepover, the amusement park at 6) | Still, or 10 to 20 s | Sends |
| Side quest | A real dark street map, a pin, a mission card with one of our cast ("Maya will appreciate this") | Still 4:5, story 9:16 | Saves, local reach |
| Real-photo post | Openly licensed photos (Unsplash, Pexels, Wikimedia Commons), one line of text. Licence and credit saved in `sources.md` | Still or carousel | Reach without animation |
| One moving thing | A still frame where only one thing moves: the notification, the record, the caption | 6 to 12 s loop | Cheap volume |
| Weekend carousel | Cover with the hook, one real event per slide, last slide asks which one | 6 to 10 slides, 1080x1350 | Saves, sends |
| Stories | Stills, 5 s clips, polls | 3 to 6 a day | Keep followers |

How many of each a week, and which run makes them: `runs.md` (3 to 5 posts a day on each network, 3 to 10 stories a
day; one heavy run on Monday, two light ones).

Two running series, every release whole on its own: **Nio builds PXI** (to camera) and **The mess and the
scrapbook**.

## Trends

Each week pick up to two things everyone is posting about and make our version. Borrow the format, never the
property: no characters, logos, fonts or music from a game, show or brand, and our own cast in every role. Nothing
about a real person's private life. A public moment (a launch, a keynote, a meme) is fine when the joke is on us.

## Hooks (first 2 seconds; never the same type twice in a row)

A question ("why does everyone leave at 1:40?"), a mid-story cold open that rewinds, a myth check ("nothing to do
in Boston on a Tuesday"), POV, only-here (real listing), a ranked list, an identity quiz, a mystery object.
No logo, no title card, no greeting.

## Endings (exactly one)

A one-word question ("what's your group chat doing tonight?"), what did we miss, a send prompt ("send this to the
friend who always says idk"), a follow reason ("the weekend map drops every Thursday"), or a save prompt.

## Captions

First line repeats the hook. Name the city and the event type in words. Say what PXI is for in the words people
search: "app to find things to do near me", "shared photo album app", "get everyone's photos from the party".
Instagram up to 5 hashtags, TikTok 3 to 5. No links or codes in TikTok captions; the link is in the bio.

## Audio

The file carries the voice and small sound effects. Instagram reels get a licensed library track at scheduling
(Metricool `audioConfiguration`, search by word, keep the voice on top: track 15 to 25). TikTok photo posts:
`autoAddMusic`. TikTok videos keep their own sound; a trending sound is added by hand in the app. Never AI music.

## Reading the numbers (Sunday's run, `runs.md`)

Sends per reach, trial-reel skip rate, completion, saves, comments, follows per post and profile visits. Keep the
hook and format of the top 2, drop the bottom 2, change one thing a week (hook, ending, format, length, time). One
line per test in `STATE.md`. Judge the strategy after 30 posts; never delete a low performer.

Human jobs to flag, never fake: replying to comments in the first hour, commenting on local venues and organizers,
asking organizers for Collabs and photo permission.
