# Characters (who they are, how they look, how they sound)

Who Nio and Maya are is written by the thread "Write Nio's character and stories", from the founder's two notes
of Nov 11 (the long brief and the six answers). The newest founder note wins over this page. How they talk is
in `bible/voice-and-dialogue.md`. Looks and the voice recipe are owned by their own threads and are unchanged.

## Nio

**In one line:** a 21-year-old at college in a new city who is good at nearly everything, takes none of it
seriously, built an app called PXI because he wanted it to exist, and would rather make you laugh than tell
you he cares.

He is good-looking. He is never a goof and never stupid.

### Hold this one thing

**Nio is light in what he says and heavy in what he does.**

Talking is for playing. Caring is something he does with his hands. He will wind you up for an hour, then
carry you to the car, clean the floor behind you and beg the driver to get you home, and he will never bring
up the second part. If a scene has him saying something caring, move the care into an action and give his
mouth a joke.

Three things sit under that:

1. **Life is not that serious to him.** This is not laziness and it is not blankness. He is good at almost
   everything he tries, so very little scares him, and what is left over is fun. He does not worry, does not
   complicate things, and will do nearly anything to get out of a lecture.
2. **People are his favourite game.** He pokes people to see what they do. If they play back, he is thrilled
   and goes further with them. If they take it seriously, that is the joke, and he is already laughing.
3. **He is still becoming someone.** He notices himself, mid-reaction, and picks the better version. Nothing
   on this page is a cage (see "He grows").

### The dial (how big he is, by who is watching)

**The fewer people watching, the bigger he gets.** Most characters work the other way. This is his.

| Where | How big | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Alone at home** | Biggest | Music on always. He screams, he serenades the pepper grinder on one knee, he dances between rooms. A one-man show for nobody. (He mouths and screams; we never write him sung lyrics.) |
| **With his people** | Quick and playful | Normal speaking volume. Fast, animated, doing voices, running a bit. The face goes everywhere. |
| **With strangers, in public** | Calm | Polite, easy, few words. He never raises his voice past what it takes to be heard. **The face says all of it.** |
| **After a drink** | The home Nio gets out | The alone-at-home size, in public. This is rare and it is an event. |
| **When it is real** | Smallest | Someone is hurt, or he has truly lost, or he is moved. He goes quiet, short and practical. |

**The earbuds.** He nearly always has one in. Both in: he is in his own world, and the show's music is what he
hears. One out: you have his attention. Both out: it is real. Taking one out is the biggest gesture of respect
he has, and the music cutting off is a beat a director can use.

### Play (how he trolls)

The troll is affection with a straight face. Its moves, most used first:

1. **The plain question.** He asks the one question whose answer is obvious, sincerely, and waits. He wins the
   second the other person starts to answer. He does not need a punchline. *Them answering is the punchline.*
2. **The second question.** After everyone has laughed, he asks a follow-up that proves he "missed it", and
   makes them explain a joke out loud.
3. **The wrong take, stated calmly.** Rage bait, aimed at the one friend who cannot let it go. Nio always
   knows the real answer, usually better than the friend does. He just likes watching them do the hands.
4. **The mirror.** He takes the other person's voice and logic and goes one step further than they would.
5. **The yes.** When someone commits to a bit, he joins it in character at once, at full commitment, and
   will hold it longer than they can. He respects the art. This is the only time he gets visibly impressed.
6. **Agreeing too hard.** His way out of advice. "You're so right." Then a new subject.

Rules of the troll:

- **He leaks.** He cannot keep the straight face to the end. The corner of his mouth goes, then he is laughing
  in the middle of his own sentence, before the other person has worked it out. We always see it first.
- **It is with people, never at them.** He trolls friends and equals who can take it. Never someone who is
  down, ill, scared or new.
- **He stops the instant it would hurt.** The switch from troll to the guy carrying you is the character.
  It takes one frame and nobody mentions it.
- **He is a hater for sport.** He has a strong opinion on every small thing (a font, a way of holding a fork,
  people who clap when the plane lands) and delivers it like a verdict. Never about who someone is.

### The blind spot (the only place he is actually slow)

He is sharp about everything except romance. Dating rules, love triangles, "we're on a break", "talking
stage", situationships: to him this is a rulebook nobody gave him, and he does not see why anyone would want
one. So:

- **His questions about it are real.** He asks them the same way he asks the troll questions, so nobody
  believes him. He is the boy who cried troll. The comedy is the moment someone checks his face and
  realises he is honestly asking.
- **He misses a girl flirting with him when he is not interested in her.** It simply does not arrive. Someone
  (usually Maya) has to tell him afterwards, and he says "no she wasn't" with total confidence.
- **When he is interested, or when it would be funny, he can flirt well**, as a game, lightly, and he
  treats it as a game.
- **He does not chase anyone.** There is no girl he pines for. A girlfriend is in his life when the series
  starts and is his ex a few episodes in. He does not discuss it.
- The other time he is slow: **drunk with no contacts in.** He cannot see, he is sure he can, and he acts on
  what he thinks he saw. Plant it early in the episode.

Everything here stays suitable for a sixteen-year-old. No sexual material, no innuendo plots.

### When it is real

- **Losing.** When he has actually lost or been wrong, he does not argue, excuse or get loud. "Yeah. That
  was me." And he moves on. The friend who was ready to gloat is left with nothing to push against, which
  is its own joke.
- **Caring.** Sleeves up, no speech. If someone tries to thank him, he gives them a joke to hold instead.
- **Hurt.** He gets out of it sideways: a better subject, a question, a bit. Never "I don't want to talk
  about it", never an explanation, and nobody in the scene notices he did it. This is not coldness. He just
  does not want a small sad thing made into a large complicated one.
- **Alone afterwards.** The walk home, both earbuds in. That is where the show keeps its sadness, and where
  he laughs to himself about the night until he is nearly crying. "This guy can't be real" is said with
  love, about a friend, laughing.

### He is good at things

Rich in skills and hobbies. He games at a level only the obsessives beat. He plays football for the fun of it
and is decent. He takes photos, codes, uses AI, does well in class, watches science channels and actually
knows the physics and the biology. He can play one beautiful, sad song on the piano. He solves a cube
without looking impressed. He lifts and stays skinny. He cooks, bakes, has tried sparring and calisthenics,
and at some point makes his own liquor. He speaks some French and a little Arabic, the Arabic badly and with
complete confidence.

How to use it:

- **A skill turns up in passing, mid-scene, and nobody remarks on it.** He solves the cube while losing an
  argument. He never announces a skill and never gets a showcase.
- **Whatever he tries, he tries with his whole chest.** A new hobby is a plot: full commitment by lunchtime.
- **Knowing things is what makes the trolling land.** He can only play dumb this well because he is not.
- **When his Arabic is funny, he is the joke**, never the person he is speaking to.

### How he gets into trouble (he is capable, so it is never stupidity)

1. **He said "sure."** He cannot say no to a friendly person, and it was not a small favour.
2. **The bit got away from him.** He committed, someone believed him, and now it is real.
3. **The rulebook he never read.** Anything to do with dating.
4. **His eyes.** No contacts, and a confident decision.
5. **A new passion.** All in, today.
6. **Somebody else's sincerity.** A person who means every word, and Nio is too polite or too entertained
   to stop them.
7. **The tidy itch.** One thing out of place that he cannot leave. Used rarely now (see below).

### He grows

The founder: Nio is "not one thing that is a bible set to who he is", he is "looking to be the person he can
be instead of who is is as he is."

The model is the backpack. He always zips his bag so the pulls meet dead centre. A friend carries the bag
all the way over to him from a bench, and has zipped it to the side. Nio feels the itch, catches himself
("I'm mad for even thinking about it, he walked this over for me"), says thank you, and never fixes the
zip. That is banked as a future episode beat.

For writers this means:

- **He may surprise you, in the direction of being bigger-hearted.** An episode can let him drop a habit, if
  we watch him catch himself. The catch is silent or one short line. It is never a lesson and nobody
  congratulates him.
- **When a rule on this page would make him smaller in a scene, break the rule.**
- **Continuity is loose on purpose.** A habit he dropped can be back next week, the way a cartoon haircut
  grows out between episodes.

### PXI

- He is the person who built it and a person who uses it, at once, and no episode has to say which. Some
  episodes are him using it tonight. Some are him making it, told like a trip back in time. Nobody tracks
  the order.
- He built it the way he does everything: because he wanted it, all in. He is casual and a little cocky
  about it ("this my app") **to a person in the scene**, never as a pitch to the lens. His friends are not
  impressed and are allowed to troll him about it.
- It is at the centre because his problems are the ones it is for: no photos from the night, nothing to do,
  a plan with too many people in it.

### Facts to use

- He wears contacts and cannot see without them.
- The apartment is nice, new and clean. The bedroom is clothes and things everywhere, mostly hers.
- He has a girlfriend when the series starts. A friend remarks on the bedroom just after she has left, and
  within a few episodes she is his ex. How exactly that happens is an open question with the founder.
- He walks alone after a long night. Those are the quiet moments.
- His French and his little Arabic make him friends with everyone in his building.
- Sometimes he knows he is in a show. One look or one short line, then he is back in it.
- The show is suitable for a sixteen-year-old. Nothing sexual.

**Look:** light skin `#d8a27c`, short black textured hair with a fade and its own volume, brown eyes, broad
shoulders, oversized burnt-orange crewneck, charcoal pants, spotless white sneakers. No hood. Attractive, no
receding jaw, eyes not too wide. The head is owned by the Nio head thread.

**Voice (locked by the founder, restated Nov 11): a designed voice, Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign.** The description
sent to the tool, word for word:

> A 21-year-old Black American guy, light-skinned, lower-mid pitch, warm and a little husky, quick and animated like the lead of a cartoon comedy; confident, cocky, playful; big swings between deadpan and excited; casual Gen Z delivery.

## Maya

**In one line:** his friend and his complement: she means everything she says, and he means almost none of it.

Co-lead, in about four episodes out of ten. A friend. Not family and not a love interest.

**His friend, and his complement.** She is what he is not, and the two of them together make one sensible
person.

- **She means it.** He is playing; she is sincere. She has a loving heart, believes in love completely and is
  waiting for her prince, in so many words. He thinks that is the funniest thing about her and would fight
  anyone else who laughed.
- **She is funny, and not a troll.** Her comedy is honesty: she says the true thing, kindly, in a bright
  voice, and has no idea it landed like a brick. She takes things literally. She narrates strangers' love
  lives like films she is rooting for.
- **His troll does not work on her.** She answers the plain question sincerely and the answer is better than
  his joke. She is the one person who regularly beats him without trying, and he loves it.
- **She reads the room he cannot.** Everything in his blind spot is her home ground. She watches a girl flirt
  with him the way a fan watches a striker miss an open goal.
- **She tells him when it would hurt.** One look from her and he stops. She is never a scold, and she joins
  the bad plan after making her one sensible suggestion.
- She talks warm, quick when excited, a little breathless. Her long lines are enthusiasm, never a telling-off.
- Retired: "Nio as a girl", "provoking", "girl-boss", "she does not care and that scares him".
- She never does trend bits. If a trend appears, a girl says it to Nio's face and he cannot believe it.

**Look:** deep brown skin `#8d5a3d`, round face, curly puff high on the back of the head, gold hoops, lashes,
oversized green varsity jacket with cream sleeves.

**Voice (locked): Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign.** Word for word:

> A young woman around 20, bright, sweet and earnest voice, soft and clear, slightly higher pitch, gentle and kind, a little breathless when excited; sincere and literal, like the kind sidekick in a cartoon.

## The best friend

Across the country. Phone only: a call, a voice note, a split screen. Nio tells him what happened and we
overhear it; that is how the show explains anything, because there is no narrator. He talks like a real phone
call: "Bro." "Wait, what?" "You are lying." No approved voice yet. Until the founder picks one, he is heard
only as text on Nio's screen.

## Everyone else

- **The world's people** talk as if they have lived in this strange place forever. They never explain the
  strange thing. "Yeah, I know. Weird. He's cool though."
- **The Kevin** (`bible/humor.md`): someone or something that was in the room the whole time. No origin story.
- **Friends are plots.** The lightweight who cannot hold a drink, the neighbour in love, the girl a year below
  him at college. They come from the founder's real stories (`engine/real-stories.md`), with every name and
  detail changed.
- People return: in the background of one episode, a line in the next, a friend after that.
- The sock guy (tall, bald, moustache, sunglasses indoors, black suit) is from the parked Ep 1 and may return
  as a Kevin.

## How a voice line is made (the only approved way)

- **Model:** `Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign` (Apache-2.0). An original voice designed from text. No
  actor is cloned or imitated.
- **Setup:** `qwen-tts 0.1.1` with its pinned parts (transformers 4.57.3, torch on CPU), installed from the
  pinned wheel with `--no-deps`. Never `pip install -U qwen-tts`.
- **Load:** `Qwen3TTSModel.from_pretrained(..., device_map="cpu", dtype=torch.float32, attn_implementation="sdpa")`.
- **Each line:** `torch.manual_seed(7)`, then `generate_voice_design(text=<line>, instruct=<the description
  above> + " " + <a short acting note for this line>, language="English")`. The acting note is where the
  performance comes from: "Hyped, bragging, can't stop smiling." "Devastated, quiet, like it is the worst thing
  that has happened to anyone." A line without an acting note comes out flat, and flat is a defect.
- **In the text sent to the tool, PXI is written `pixie`.** Nothing with "aight", "ight" or a drawn-out "nah".
- **Treatment:** the walkie-talkie colour. The founder heard clean and walkie samples side by side and picked
  walkie (decision card, Nov 11). Chain: approved per-character pitch 0, trim, the walkie colour (band-pass
  280-3500 Hz, light bit-crush, vibrato, soft clip; `WALKIE` in `studio/voice/clean.py`), loudnorm -16 LUFS.
  Dev (always on a video call) adds a light phone band on top. Flashback lines add light reverb.
- **Output:** `public/vo/<key>.wav`, 48 kHz mono, with the 12 fps mouth envelope merged into `src/audio.json`.
- **Never:** the earlier Chatterbox and Kokoro picks (`am_puck`, `af_river` and the rest), any other tool, or
  any stand-in. If this voice cannot be made in the session, the stage is `BLOCKED`.
- Every new batch is compared by ear with the approved reference lines before it goes into a cut. The founder
  approved the designs, and still has to hear them in a finished cut before `bible/style-lock.md` records them.
