# Story (the shape of an episode, and the tests it has to pass)

Two founder notes hold this page up. Nov 10: "i dont even know what the point of this new plot you made is i
need you to have the engine produce genuinly interesting stories." Nov 11: "i need nice funny stories so
renetion is kept and so pxi is discovered." A story here is clear, funny, and worth telling a friend.

## 1. The series in one breath

**Nio is 21, broke, and new to a city where he came for college. He kept missing everything and was in nobody's
photos, so he built an app, PXI. The series is his life there: his friends, his neighbours, the girl who
becomes his ex, the nights out and the walks home, with a world around him that is stranger than he is.**

- It is a comedy about him and his people first. An episode has to be funny to someone who has never heard of
  PXI.
- PXI is his, and it is also simply an app that exists: he is its founder and a guy using it at once, and
  nobody tracks which. It shows up when he is stuck, the way he would really use it, and his world slowly fills up
  because of it: people, places, albums, stamps. That is the journey a viewer follows.
- The founder's line for what the series is, which the tag and the pinned reel carry: "nio making a platform
  he thinks would have solved his problems and will in future and inviting us to use it."

## 2. The beat sheet, every time, even for 15 seconds

Times are for a short cut of about 35 seconds. Scale them for a longer cut and keep the order.

| Beat | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0 to 1.5 s | One picture or line that is off. Maya says something insane. A man is already in the room. |
| **Setup** | 2 to 8 s | The tiny, real problem, and a real reason to leave the apartment. It starts at home. We learn what he wants from people talking, never from a speech. |
| **Absurd turn** | 8 to 18 s | The normal problem gets weird. The Kevin appears, or the thing everyone treats as normal. |
| **Escalation** | 18 to 30 s | Worse, and everyone stays calm. A second weird thing. He tries to fix it alone and fails because other people are involved. A callback if there is one. |
| **Emotional hit** | 2 s | One real feeling. He sits down. He wipes his eyes and says "I'm just tired." He looks at a memory. It is not explained. |
| **Glitch button** | last 3 s | The picture breaks (`bible/craft-library.md`, Glitch button), then cut. No resolution. A standalone ends on its weirdest image. |

A story that honestly needs two releases ends its first on a real cliffhanger and says "stay tuned". It is
still never labelled "Part 1", and each release still plays whole.

## 3. Plot engines (rotate them; never the same one two episodes running)

1. **The oblivious plot.** Nio walks into a social situation, misses every signal and stays chill. We see it
   before he does. Someone has to tell him afterwards.
2. **The dismissal plot.** Someone brings up the girl, the breakup, the room. He never denies or explains. He
   pivots, and we forget the question too.
3. **The weird character plot.** Someone strange is simply there. Nio is the only one bothered. Everyone else:
   "yeah, weird, he's cool." By the end Nio is calm too. No origin story.
4. **The troll plot.** Nio winds someone up for fun, commits to it, and it gets away from him.
5. **The can't-say-no plot.** Someone asks him for a small favour. He says "sure", and it is not small.
6. **The friend plot.** A friend is the problem tonight and Nio is the one who gets him home.
7. **The dream.** Uncanny. It can be eerie, funny, sad or truly devastating. No PXI and no punchline needed.
8. **The after-party** is an ending, not an episode: the mess, the quiet, the drink on the couch, he sits, the
   camera pulls wide, a man gets up from under the bean bag, Nio does not turn his head, "they left."

More shapes, taken from how Gumball builds plots, are in `bible/gumball-lens.md`.

## 4. PXI in the story

The founder's brief: "Never force PXI. Ask: can PXI fit comfortably without forced placement? If no, leave it
out." And the founder's aim: "so pxi is discovered", with "pxi being at the centere of it." So:

- **Ask what Nio would really reach for.** Natural fits: taking pictures (Camera, Shared Album); planning a
  trip or a night (group plans, the event, the invite link); finding something to do (the Discover map); a
  sweet or sad moment (a notification, a look-back, a memory); something of his blowing up (Thread, Wall).
- **Three honest answers.** *Engine:* his way out of the problem is a PXI feature. *Moment:* PXI appears once,
  used the way a person would. *None:* it does not fit, so it is not in the picture.
- **The mix.** Most episodes have an engine or a moment. Holiday episodes always have an engine, because they
  model the day (`engine/calendar.md`). Dreams, fillers and at most one episode in four may have none.
- **It is shown and never sold.** Nobody explains the app in a scene. The most Nio says is "this my app, yo."
- **The ask lives outside the story.** Every release carries its call to action in the tag, the caption or the
  end frame (`engine/formats.md`), including the ones where PXI is not in the picture.
- **The product fit test.** Would a real person use PXI exactly like this, here, tonight?

## 5. The tests a premise has to pass

One line each. If a line is hard to write, the premise is not ready.

1. **The sentence.** Nio wants X, but Y weird thing happens, and he responds by Z.
2. **Funny without PXI.** Tell it without the app. Is it still a story someone would retell?
3. **Recognition.** Is there a moment in it the viewer has lived this month?
4. **A want you can film.** Small, real and visible. Get the friend home. Get one photo. Find the event.
5. **The weird thing.** What is the Kevin, and can everyone stay calm about it?
6. **The engine.** Does it keep producing trouble without help? Three steps should come easily.
7. **The feeling.** One true feeling, hit once for two seconds, never said aloud.
8. **PXI.** Engine, moment or none, by section 4.
9. **The retell and the send.** One sentence a viewer would say to a friend, and who they send it to.

## 6. The tests a script has to pass

- **The cold read.** A reader who knows nothing reads only the short-cut script and says who this is, what he
  wanted, what happened, what the strange thing was, what he felt at the end, and where they laughed. If they
  cannot say what he wanted or what happened, it fails. If they cannot name a laugh, it fails.
- **Sound off.** The same from the pictures and the subtitles alone. Most people meet the show muted.
- **No homework.** It needs nothing from an earlier episode. Lore rewards followers and never punishes
  strangers.
- **No dead air.** Every shot has something to watch: a face changing, something moving, a detail to find.
- **Ends somewhere new**, written into `bible/timeline.md`: a person he now knows, a place, an album, a plant.

## 7. The journey

- **Chapters** the viewer can hold. First: new here, a girl, nobody else. Then the girl is gone and the circle
  starts to fill. Then his thing becomes the place to be. The founder names and reorders the chapters.
- **The girl.** She is there from the start. Within the first few episodes a friend remarks on the bedroom
  (clothes and things everywhere, mostly hers) just after she has left, and she is his ex. He never discusses it. It surfaces in dismissals and on walks.
- What visibly grows and never resets: his circle, the albums he is in, his stamps, the places that know him.
- People return. Lore questions go in the caption for the comments.
- **Nio changes.** He is not a fixed list of traits. He drops a habit when life asks him to (the backpack zip,
  `engine/real-stories.md` R3).

## 8. Real stories

The best premises are things that happened. The founder drops stories in chat; `engine/story-intake.md` says
how they become premises. A real story is inspiration and never a transcript: names, places and details
change, and nobody real can be recognised.

## 9. The two pinned reels

- **The pilot:** one complete episode that passes everything above. A stranger finishes it knowing who Nio is,
  having laughed, and wanting the next one.
- **"What this is":** Nio, 30 seconds or less, telling us what he built and why, as himself: alive, cocky,
  funny, to a friend on the phone or straight to us. It is the one place the show explains itself.

## 10. Warnings from the pilots (keep)

- **"Not Me" (Nov 10):** nobody could tell who Nio was or what he wanted, the stake was private embarrassment,
  PXI only sent a notification, and it was shown without sound.
- **"No Evidence" (Nov 11):** the fix for the above was to have Nio state his problem and plan to the camera.
  That made him "talk like an npc". The plot ("calling a guy you are the actaully guy") was "weird and corny".
  The faces did nothing, shots sat on people and walls, and it was "not funny". Clear is not enough. The want
  comes out of people talking, and the episode has to be funny.
