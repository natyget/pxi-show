# The world (Maya, the friends, the places, and a first slate of stories)

Written by the thread "Write Nio's character and stories". The cast is built the way `bible/gumball-style.md`
section 4 says: **each person is one clear idea plus one worry, runs on their own logic, and can carry an
episode alone.** Wherever the founder told a real story about someone, that person is the seed and the
detail is his. Everyone else is new. No one here is taken from any existing show.

**Every name is a working name for the founder to change.** Looks are one hook each, for the design thread.
Nobody is the joke for where they are from, how they sound, their body or their health.

## The two leads

**Nio.** `bible/characters.md`. In this cast he is the one who is playing.

**Maya.** `bible/characters.md`. In this cast she is the one who means it. More about her, for stories:

- *Her idea:* she believes every love story she has ever seen, and is waiting for hers with a checklist.
- *Her worry:* that she is "too much", which makes her apologise for exactly the things people like her for.
- *Her logic:* everything is a sign. A dropped glove is the start of something.
- *What she is great at:* reading people. She knows who likes whom from across a room, and is right.
- *What she is terrible at:* her own. She will not notice the nice ordinary boy because he did not arrive on
  a horse.
- *With Nio:* she is the only person his plain questions do not work on. He asks, she answers honestly, and
  the honest answer is funnier. He teases the prince; anyone else who does has a problem with Nio.

## His people

**Sami, the lightweight.** *(From the founder's story R1.)*
- *His idea:* dignity above all. He would rather pass out standing up than be seen sitting down.
- *His worry:* that someone, somewhere, saw.
- *His logic:* if nobody caught it, it did not happen. He will stand perfectly upright for exactly as long as
  the door is open.
- *How he talks:* formal when it is going badly. "I am completely fine, thank you for asking."
- *Hook:* always slightly overdressed. Trousers with a pattern you have to squint at.
- *Carries an episode:* the morning after, from his side, reconstructing a night he will swear went well.

**Theo, the one who calls it.** *(From the founder's troll note: the friend who predicts the film.)*
- *His idea:* he has a prediction for everything, delivered as fact: the film, the match, the weather, your
  relationship.
- *His worry:* being asked "have you seen it?"
- *His logic:* being early is the same as being right.
- *How he talks:* in percentages. "Ninety percent she texts back by nine."
- *With Nio:* he is the perfect target for the calm wrong take, because he cannot let one go, and he uses
  his hands when he explains.
- *Hook:* always holding a drink he is not drinking, to point with.
- *Carries an episode:* one of his predictions comes true and he has no idea what to do next.

**Benji, the kid who helps.** *(From the founder's backpack story.)*
- *His idea:* he does thoughtful things for people, slightly wrong, with his whole heart.
- *His worry:* being in the way.
- *His logic:* if he saw it and could have helped, it is already his job.
- *How he talks:* in updates. "I moved your bike. It's safer now. It's on the roof."
- *With Nio:* he is who Nio grows around. You cannot be annoyed at Benji and stay the person you want to be.
- *Hook:* a year younger, always carrying something that belongs to someone else.

**Farid, down the hall.** *(From the founder's story R2. Treated with affection.)*
- *His idea:* a romantic of the old school. Forty, good suits, better cologne, and certain that love is
  mostly a matter of presentation and nerve.
- *His worry:* that he has left it too late.
- *His logic:* if you dress for the wedding, the wedding will come.
- *How he talks:* in pronouncements, warmly, and in two languages. He corrects Nio's Arabic by repeating it
  back properly and then answering as if Nio had said it right.
- *With Nio:* the friend Nio cannot say no to. He treats Nio as a wise younger brother, which Nio is not.
- *Hook:* you smell him before he comes round the corner, and it is a good smell.
- *Care:* he never gets the girl, nobody is ever unsafe, and the show is on his side even when he is wrong.

**Dev, the best friend who is far away.** *(Name from the pilot. Waiting on the founder: who he is.)*
- *His idea:* he has known Nio longest and is the least impressed person alive.
- *His worry:* that Nio is having more fun without him. He would never say so.
- *His logic:* nothing Nio tells him happened until there is a photo.
- *How he talks:* like a real call. Eating. Half listening. One exact question at the worst moment.
- *Hook:* always on a screen, always mid-meal, a different meal every time.

**mango_dad, the one who beats him.** *(New. From the founder's "only hardcore people are better".)*
- *His idea:* the only player Nio cannot beat at the game he is best at. He has never spoken on mic.
- *The fact:* he is eleven, and he has a bedtime.
- *His logic:* he is not trying to humble anyone. He is just better, and he has to go now, his mum is calling.
- *With Nio:* the one place Nio is plainly, repeatedly beaten, and he takes it the way the founder says he
  does: humble, quiet, "good game." Then he tries to learn from a child.
- *Hook:* we know him by a username and one green hoodie.

**Dr. Okafor, who has advice.** *(New. From the founder's "avoiding being given lectures and advice".)*
- *Her idea:* a brilliant lecturer who has decided Nio has potential and intends to tell him about it.
- *Her worry:* that nobody takes notes any more.
- *Her logic:* any conversation is thirty seconds from becoming a life lesson.
- *With Nio:* the nearest thing he has to a nemesis. She is always right, which is the problem. He will do
  almost anything to leave the room before the lesson arrives, and she is very patient.
- *Hook:* she can appear at the end of any corridor.

**The ex.** She is in the first episodes and gone a few in. *Waiting on the founder for who she is and how it
ends.* Until then she is written warmly and is never the villain. Most of the mess in the bedroom is hers.

**Imane, a year below.** *(From R2.)* The sanest person in the series. Her whole logic: "No." Nio trusts her
judgement more than his own and has never told her.

## The places (the map is the world)

Every place a story goes to stays on Nio's map afterwards. That is how the world, and what PXI is, grows
without anyone explaining it.

- **His apartment.** Clean front room, the fridge, the couch. The bedroom door is usually shut.
- **The building.** Thin walls, a lift that takes too long, a lobby where Farid holds court.
- **Campus.** The corridor where Dr. Okafor appears. The bench the backpack was left on.
- **The pitch.** Five-a-side, Thursday. He is decent and the most entertained person on it.
- **The gym.** He lifts hard and stays skinny, and has a theory about why.
- **The corner shop.** Open at any hour. The man behind the counter has seen everything and remarks on
  nothing.
- **Night.** The market, someone's party, the walk home alone.

**Real events (founder, Nov 12).** Where a story sends Nio out to something on the map, prefer an event that
is really on PXI that week over an invented one, when one fits the story. It is not required, and the story
is never bent to fit a listing. Two things people actually search for are the two things Nio most often needs
in a plot: an app where everyone's photos from the night land in one album, and something to do near him
tonight. A story that turns on either of those has set up its own caption.

## A first slate (twelve stories; each is one ordinary thing)

Format: the one-line premise, whose ego drives it, the kind of story (`bible/gumball-style.md` section 2), and
where PXI sits. All are CANDIDATE until the founder approves them in `engine/premise-bank.md`.

1. ~~No Pics~~ retired Nov 13 by the founder. Season 1 starts from `season-1/series-plan.md` instead.
2. **Tiger.** Nio has to get Sami home, cannot see without his contacts, and books the wrong ride. *Ego:*
   Sami's dignity. *Kind:* quest. *PXI:* moment, next morning's album, Sami upright in every photo. *From R1.*
3. **The Zip.** Benji returns Nio's bag from across campus, zipped wrong. *Ego:* Nio's itch, and him choosing
   not to scratch it. *Kind:* small habit. *PXI:* none. The quiet one.
4. **A Break.** Maya explains that two friends are "on a break". Nio's honest questions spread through the
   group until nobody can say what the rules are, including the couple. *Ego:* nobody believes he is really
   asking. *Kind:* small thing balloons. *PXI:* moment, the group album where two people stop appearing
   together.
5. **The Hands.** Nio tells Theo the moon is closer than they say. Theo makes slides. *Ego:* Theo cannot let
   it go, Nio cannot stop. *Kind:* the leads fall out. *PXI:* engine, Theo puts "the moon talk" on the map as
   an event and sixty strangers come.
6. **Good Game.** Nio finally meets mango_dad, at mango_dad's birthday party. *Ego:* Nio's, quietly set
   aside. *Kind:* side character. *PXI:* engine, the invite came from the kid's mum.
7. **The Suit.** Farid is in love with someone he saw on a train and needs Nio's help getting ready. *Ego:*
   Farid's certainty. *Kind:* a friend's pride drives it. *PXI:* moment or none. *From R2.*
8. **Batch One.** Nio takes up fermenting. By evening the building treats his kitchen as a bar and him as its
   landlord. *Ego:* whole-chest commitment. *Kind:* balloons. *PXI:* engine, a tasting on the map.
   (For a 16+ audience this may need to be ginger beer or hot sauce; the founder decides.)
9. **Nothing On.** Nio tells Dev the city is dead while a parade, a wedding and a fire-eater pass behind him.
   *Ego:* he has decided, so he will not look. *Kind:* small thing. *PXI:* engine, the map, opened last.
10. **Potential.** Dr. Okafor wants a word. Nio spends a day escaping one conversation. *Ego:* his dread of a
    lecture against her patience. *Kind:* authority. *PXI:* moment, he goes to every event on the map to be
    somewhere she is not, and she is at the last one.
11. **Open Goal.** A girl is plainly into Nio. He thinks she wants photography tips. Maya watches. *Ego:*
    none; this is his blind spot. *Kind:* romance (rare). *PXI:* moment, she makes a shared album for two
    and he adds Theo.
12. **Upright.** The night of "Tiger", told from Sami's side, in which he was magnificent. *Ego:* Sami's.
    *Kind:* side character, same night seen again. *PXI:* engine, the album disagrees with him photo by photo.

Mix across the twelve: three small things, two that balloon, two side characters, two friend-driven, one
quest, one authority, one romance. That is close to the spread in the show we studied.
