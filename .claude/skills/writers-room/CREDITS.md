# Credits

The writers' room is written in our own words, drawing on ideas from:

- sb-dev/narrative-production-skills (MIT): concept alternatives differ in story logic; approval becomes a constraint;
  evaluators never rewrite; smallest-scope revisions.
- jtydhr88/screenwriting-skills (MIT, skill text): premise as a joke machine; setup/punch hygiene; runners; comedic
  modes per character; Reiss's test; dialogue checks (cover the names, no telling what's known, no agreeing).
- eternityspring/shuohao-skills (Apache-2.0): runtime estimation gated at ±15%; hook in the first beats; shots claim
  beats; line-fits-shot.
- 0xhughs/director-skills (MIT): one purpose and one subject per shot; one camera move; continuity tracking.
- danjdewhurst/story-skills (MIT): isolated reader personas; quote exact lines; don't pad.
- ccf/humanize, shir-danishyar/humanize, blader/humanizer, ameenmo/humanize-skill (MIT): AI-writing tells, preserve
  mode, audit-then-fix, burstiness re-check.
- talsraviv/comedy-writers-room (no licence): only the idea of separate audience critics; no text used.
- Peters & Chin-Yee 2025 (doi:10.1098/rsos.241776) and Muñoz-Ortiz et al. 2024 (PMC11422446): LLM text overgeneralises
  and flattens sentence length and emotional range.
