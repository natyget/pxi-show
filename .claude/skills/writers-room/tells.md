# AI tells to strip from dialogue and scripts

One hit isn't slop; a pile of them is. Quote the line for each hit.

**Explaining**
- Narrating what the picture already shows ("that's me sneezing").
- Telling another character what both already know.
- Explaining the joke, or a line after the punch that restates it.
- Stating or debating the theme; a closing moral; "I guess we learned…"; an epilogue after the climax.
- Broad general statements where a specific concrete detail would do.

**Shape**
- Tidy symmetry, mirrored lines, rule-of-three lists, synonym cycling, saying one thing three ways.
- "It's not X, it's Y." Staccato drama fragments ("One phone. Three photos.").
- One-line kicker closers on every scene. Every line having a punchline.
- Uniform line length. (Vary it: a one-word line, a run-on, a silence.)
- Each beat being the most likely next beat.

**Voice**
- Staged run-ups: "Okay, here's the thing", "Honestly?", "Listen".
- Nervous self-narration in tidy sentences ("totally normal person thing").
- Arguing with an objection nobody raised.
- Characters agreeing with each other; everyone sounding alike (cover the names: can you tell who's talking?).
- Filler: "I mean", "you know", "literally", "absolutely", "vibe", "chaos".
- Ellipses and dashes as a crutch for timing (use a take instead).
- Only joy and surprise. Let them be petty, scared, grossed out, jealous.
- The lead being right, calm, articulate in a crisis, or vindicated.
