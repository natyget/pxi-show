Script: `episodes/office-hours/script.md` (season 1, episode 1, from `episodes/season-1/episodes-v2.md`; lint
passes). 24 fps, 9:16. **Long cut about 82 s** (every shot). **Short cut about 71 s** (S shots only), which is
the shortest the script plays at the pacing floors. The script asks for about 60 s, so reaching 60 means cutting
words, not frames: the writer's call.

**Where people are.** Lecture hall: Prof H at the lectern, bottom centre, the mic on a gooseneck. Nio in row 4,
aisle seat, screen right. Theo on his left. The empty seat on Theo's left. The cap girl in row 3, in front of Nio.
Waiting room: chairs along the left wall, ticket machine by the entrance on the right, the inner door at the far
end with NOW SERVING above it and the clock beside it. Darnell in the chair nearest the inner door, the spinner
boy and the flower-crown girl next to him. Nio takes the empty chair by the entrance. Office: Prof H behind the
desk, Nio in the visitor chair facing him, the door behind Nio.

**In hands.** Prof H: nothing (his laptop shuts in 1). Nio: his phone from 15 on, back in his pocket at 18.
Theo: a pen. Darnell: his ticket (4) in his left hand, the lab report on his knees. The cleaner: a mop.

**Faces** are the rig's named presets (`studio/anim/src/nw3d/face.ts`). Mouths follow the audio (`src/lips`).

## PXI

The map in 16 is the impossible thing: the app shows where he really is. It is the real Discover map view
(`src/components/discover/map/`: DiscoverMap, mapStyle, mapLook, MapPin, MapCarousel, PosterFallback), drawn by
`tools/pxi/screens.py map`. Two changes from the script so it matches the app 1:1: the viewer's dot is PXI's light
purple, not blue, and the map card has no attendee count, so "1 going" becomes the card's own when line, ALWAYS
(the title and the venue do the rest). The call to action is in the caption.

## Voices

Nio: the approved voice. **Prof H, Theo, Darnell, the cleaner, and none of the others have an approved voice.**
Lines and acting notes are in `studio/voice/jobs_office-hours.json`. Prof H's whisper is recorded as a whisper and
made to boom by the room (a big PA reverb), not by shouting.

## Pacing check

Every shot that must be understood is 1.2 s or longer, except the two montage stair angles in 15 (18 f each)
and the sprint smear in 19 (12 f), which are action. Speech is at most 2.3 words a second (6, 26, 33 sit at the
ceiling). The plaque is held 3.3 s and the map 3 s. Subtitles carry every line.
