# Office Hours v5 (Nov 17): rebuilt on the Gumball engine

What v4 was missing, measured against Gumball season 6: **the double act.** Every Gumball scene is the lead
and his sincere other half pulling in opposite directions, fast, over each other. Theo cannot do that; Maya
can. So Maya loves office hours, and drags him. Also added: the lead's **theory as a cutaway in another
style** (his imagined version, played as a film trailer), which the real world then turns out to match
exactly; and **everyone else's different logic**, calm (Maya chats with Darnell like it's normal).

Keeps from v4: the want spoken early, the ticket joke, the betrayal, the flyer, the door on the pitch,
"Darnell's in goal". Sets reused, plus the trailer cutaway (same corridor, grainy and red).
No music: dialogue and sound effects only.

```
[INT. LECTURE HALL, 4:52 PM. NIO in football kit under his desk, ball between his boots. Clock: 4:52. Beside
him, MAYA, notebook full of colour-coded tabs. PROF H, enormous and soft, closes his laptop. His whisper booms
through the mic.]

PROF H: Nio! Can you swing by my office hours after class? It won't take a minute.
[Maya gasps and grabs Nio's arm.]
MAYA: He picked you. I go every week. He has a candle.
NIO (low): Maya. The final's at five. If I'm not on that pitch, we forfeit.
MAYA: It's one minute!
NIO: That's how they get you. It's one minute, then you're forty, and it's still office hours.
MAYA: That's not a real thing.

[CUT TO: NIO'S IMAGINATION. A film trailer, grainy, red, a deep trailer voice made of Nio's own voice.]
NIO (trailer voice): In a world... where one minute... is forever.
[A corridor that never ends. A clock with no hands. A man with a beard to his belt, holding a ticket.]
NIO (trailer voice): Office Hours. Now showing. Always.
[BACK TO THE HALL. Maya is staring at him.]
MAYA: Did you just do a trailer?
THEO (behind them): That's what Darnell said.                              RECORDED
NIO: Who's Darnell?                                                         RECORDED
[Theo turns, slowly, to the seat beside him. Dust. A cobweb. A name card: DARNELL.]
NIO (beat, getting up): I'm going out the back.
MAYA (getting up, delighted): I'll come!

[INT. CORRIDOR. Nio speed-walks toward the EXIT sign. Maya skips beside him. They pass Prof H's open door.
Again. Again. The EXIT sign never gets closer. Fluorescent hum.]
MAYA: Oh, I love this hallway.
NIO (checking the PXI map: his purple dot inside a building that isn't on campus, "ALWAYS · Office Hours"):
It's the trailer. We're in the trailer.
MAYA: You're being dramatic.
CLEANER (mopping, not looking up): Nobody goes past it, baby.
[The door swings wide by itself. Maya walks straight in. Nio's phone: 4:57. He goes in after her.]

[INT. WAITING ROOM. Plastic chairs. A ticket machine. The clock with no hands, exactly like the trailer.
Students waiting with papers on their knees, and DARNELL, beard to his belt, very calm.]
MAYA (lighting up): Darnell! How's the thesis?
DARNELL (warm): Hey, Maya. I'm next.
[Nio stares at Maya. Maya waves at Darnell like this is a coffee shop. Nio takes a ticket: 4,812. The sign
above the door: NOW SERVING 3.]
NIO (to Darnell, quietly): How long have you been next?
[Darnell starts counting on his fingers. Runs out. Gives up.]
DARNELL: Since freshman year.
MAYA (sincerely, to Nio): See? It moves.

[The inner door opens. PROF H fills it sideways, beaming.]
PROF H: Nio! Come on in!
[Every head in the room turns to Nio, betrayed. Darnell's ticket falls out of his hand. Maya gives Nio two
thumbs up.]

[INT. OFFICE. Tiny. A candle. Prof H folded behind the desk. He slides over a flyer, a hand-drawn picture
of himself on a stage: "IMPROV! THURSDAY 7PM"]
PROF H (very small): Would you come? Be honest.
[Nio looks at the flyer. At Prof H's hopeful face. At the clock on the wall: 5:00.]
NIO: ...Yeah. I'll come.

[Prof H lights up like a child. The wall behind him swings open like a door: grass, sun, a whistle.]

[EXT. PITCH, 5:00 PM. A door standing on its own in the middle of the pitch. Nio steps out with the ball.
THEO stares at the door, at the little office behind it, at Nio.]
THEO: ...You made it.
[Behind Nio, the whole waiting room walks out onto the grass, squinting at the sun. Maya, last but one,
waves at everyone. Last of all, DARNELL, beard blowing in the wind, walks straight into goal and pulls on
a pair of gloves.]
NIO (dropping the ball for kick-off): Darnell's in goal.
```
