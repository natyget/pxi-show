## 1. Office Hours

Where: a lecture hall, a corridor that loops, a waiting room, a tiny office, the pitch · Chore: go to office
hours · Dodge: Nio decides they are optional · The impossible thing: office hours are a place, and the
people who skipped them are still in it · Skill that saves him: he cannot say no to people ·
PXI: the map shows a building that isn't on campus · Eerie: Darnell · About 60 s

**Professor Haverbrook ("Prof H")**, new to the cast: an enormous, soft man in a cardigan with tiny round
glasses who speaks in a whisper the microphone turns into thunder. He is desperate for his students to think
he is cool, learns their slang from the internet and uses it nearly right. Everyone on campus treats his
office hours the way you treat a haunted house. He has no idea.

```
[INT. LECTURE HALL, 4:55 PM. Under the desks, NIO and THEO are already in football kit, boots laced. Nio
has a ball between his feet. The wall clock reads 4:55. PROF H closes his laptop. His whisper fills the room
through the mic.]
PROF H: Nio. Office hours. Today.

[Thirty heads turn. A girl in the next row slowly takes her cap off, like at a funeral.]
NIO (low, nodding at the clock): Game's at five.
THEO (eyes on the seat beside him): That's what Darnell said.
[The seat beside Theo. Empty. A name card on the desk: DARNELL. A water bottle, a planner, a thick layer of
dust.]
NIO: Who's Darnell?
THEO (barely moving his lips): Bro's not coming back.
[Nio looks at the dust. At the clock: 4:56. He scoops up the ball and slides out the side door.]

[INT. CORRIDOR. Fluorescent hum. At the far end, a door, wide open, warm light pouring out. The plaque:
"PROF. HAVERBROOK. MY DOOR IS ALWAYS OPEN 🙂". Nio turns the other way. Walks. Turns a corner. The same
door, ahead of him. A little more open.]
NIO: No.
[He takes the stairs down two flights, bursts through the stairwell door: the same corridor, the same door.]
[He opens the PXI map. His light purple dot is inside a grey building that is not on campus. The event card:
"ALWAYS · Office Hours · Haverbrook Hall · FREE". The dot is inside it.]
NIO (quietly): I did not RSVP to that.
[A CLEANER mops past, unbothered.]
CLEANER: Just go in, baby. It's faster.

[Nio sprints. SMASH CUT: he is sitting in a WAITING ROOM. Plastic chairs. A ticket machine. A clock with no
hands. The music has stopped; only the hum. Other students wait with papers on their knees: a boy with a
fidget spinner, a girl in a flower crown, and DARNELL, beard to his belt, very calm, holding a lab report
with a coffee ring on it. Above the inner door: NOW SERVING 3.]

DARNELL: First time?
NIO: I'm not even here.
DARNELL (nodding, kind): Yeah. We all were.
[Nio looks at the ticket in Darnell's hand. 4.]
DARNELL (gently, like it's nothing): Did they ever drop the new GTA?
[A long beat. Nio cannot do it to him.]
NIO: ...Not yet, bro.
[Darnell nods, satisfied, and goes back to waiting.]

[The door opens. PROF H fills it sideways, delighted.]
PROF H: Nio! Come in, come in.

[INT. OFFICE. Tiny. Prof H folded behind the desk. A HANG IN THERE cat poster. A bobblehead of himself.]
PROF H: So. I wanted a word.
[Hold. Room tone. Nio, sweating.]
[Prof H slides a flyer across the desk. Hand-drawn. "PROF H · IMPROV · THURSDAY".]
PROF H (very small): Would you come?
[Nio looks at the flyer. At Prof H. Prof H's lip is going. A long beat.]
NIO: ...Sure.
[A single tear rolls down Prof H's face.]

[Behind Nio, the door swings open onto the sunny quad. In the waiting room the clock has hands. NOW SERVING
4. Darnell stands, squints at the sun, and walks out into it, beard first.]
PROF H: Same time next week?
[Nio is already gone. The door clicks shut by itself.]

[EXT. FIVE-A-SIDE PITCH, 5:01 PM. Nio jogs on, hair wrecked, ball under his arm.]
THEO: Where were you?
[Nio says nothing. He holds up the flyer.]
[Theo looks at it. Goes white. Takes one step back.]
```

Laughs: the cap off like a funeral / the dusty seat / "I did not RSVP to that" / "Just go in, baby. It's
faster." / Darnell's beard / "Did they ever drop the new GTA?" and "Not yet, bro" / the improv flyer / "Nobody's ever said yes"
and Darnell set free by it / "You're coming to improv Thursday".
Eerie: the corridor that loops, the clock with no hands, NOW SERVING 3, Darnell's calm. Never explained.
Caption: skipped office hours one time. Who's your Darnell?
