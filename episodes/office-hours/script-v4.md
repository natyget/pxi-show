# Office Hours v4 (back from the writers' room, Nov 17)

Fixes for the stranger's second review: by second 8 the viewer hears what Nio wants (the final at five, or the
team forfeits) and why office hours is a threat (Darnell went and never came back). Every line is said the way
a person says it. The ending pays the want off: he makes the game, through a door that should not be there.
Regular Show plot (a small chore, a dodge, the impossible thing, out fast), Gumball faces and dialogue. No
slang terms, no brand names.

Sets reused: hall with clock, ball, Darnell's name card, corridor, phone map, waiting room, office, pitch.
New picture: a door standing alone in the middle of the pitch. Lines marked RECORDED are already voiced.

```
[INT. LECTURE HALL, 4:52 PM. NIO and THEO in football kit under their desks, boots on, ball between Nio's
feet. Clock: 4:52. PROF H, enormous and soft, closes his laptop. His whisper booms through the mic.]

PROF H (friendly): Nio! Can you swing by my office hours after class? It won't take a minute.
THEO (low, fast, panicking): Bro. The final's at five. If you're not on the pitch, we forfeit.
NIO (packing up, easy): Relax. It's one minute.
THEO: That's what Darnell said.                                           RECORDED
NIO: Who's Darnell?                                                       RECORDED
[Theo turns his head, slowly, to the seat beside him. Dust. A cobweb from the chair to the desk. A name
card: DARNELL.]
NIO (beat): ...I'll be quick.

[INT. CORRIDOR. Nio walks fast toward the EXIT sign at the end. He passes Prof H's open door. Walks on.
Passes it again. And again. The EXIT sign never gets closer. His phone buzzes: THEO, "4:57 where are you"]
[The PXI map: his purple dot is inside a building that is not on campus. The card: "ALWAYS · Office Hours ·
Haverbrook Hall · FREE"]
NIO (to himself, walking faster): I'm not going in. I'm going past it.
CLEANER (mopping, not looking up): Nobody goes past it, baby.
[The door swings wide by itself.]

[SMASH CUT: WAITING ROOM. Plastic chairs. A ticket machine. A clock with no hands. Students waiting with
papers on their knees. One of them, DARNELL, has a beard down to his belt and a very calm face.]
DARNELL (kind): Hey. Take a ticket.
[Nio takes one. It says 4,812. The sign above the door says NOW SERVING 3.]
NIO: What number are you?
DARNELL (holding up a ticket, proud): Four. I'm next.
NIO: How long have you been next?
[Darnell starts counting on his fingers. Runs out of fingers. Gives up.]
DARNELL: Since freshman year.
[Nio's phone: 4:59.]

[The inner door opens. PROF H fills it sideways, beaming.]
PROF H: Nio! Come on in!
[Every head in the waiting room turns to Nio, betrayed. Darnell's ticket falls out of his hand.]

[INT. OFFICE. Tiny. Prof H folded behind the desk.]
PROF H: So. I wanted to ask you something.
[He slides over a flyer with a hand-drawn picture of himself on a stage: "IMPROV! THURSDAY 7PM"]
PROF H (very small): Would you come? Be honest.
[Nio looks at the flyer. At Prof H. At the clock on the wall: 5:00.]
NIO: ...Yeah. I'll come.

[Prof H lights up like a child. The wall behind him swings open like a door, and through it: grass, sun, a
whistle.]

[EXT. PITCH, 5:00 PM. A door is standing on its own in the middle of the pitch. Nio steps out of it, ball under
his arm. THEO, mid-warm-up, stares at the door, at the little office behind it, at Nio.]
THEO: ...You made it.
[Behind Nio, the whole waiting room walks out onto the grass, squinting at the sun. Last out is DARNELL, beard
blowing in the wind. He walks straight into goal and pulls on a pair of gloves.]
NIO (dropping the ball for kick-off): Darnell's in goal.
```
