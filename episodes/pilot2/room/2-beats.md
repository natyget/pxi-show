# Pilot (remake) · "No Evidence" · beat sheet · ≈60 s

**The point (one line):** Nio has the best night of his life at his first party in a new city, wakes up with zero
photos of it and nobody's number, and goes to absurd, procedural lengths to get the night back, which is the reason
he builds PXI.

**Why people follow:** a want anyone has had (proof that the night happened, and the girl in the green jacket), a
lonely new-in-the-city heart under the comedy, and a real cliffhanger: she has the photos, and she's coming back
Saturday. Reel 2 tells them what the show is.

True thing (never spoken): everyone is in everyone's photos, and nobody has their own.

**PXI's role:** it doesn't exist yet. This episode is why it has to. The pain (every photo of you is on someone else's
phone, and you never see them) is exactly what face match and shared albums solve. The audience gets the product
without an ad.

**Comedy check (bible/humor.md):** small social problem (no photos, no name) · a plan with logic (estimate, flyer,
door to door) · procedure (the count, the form) · polite people go along (each one helps, each photo is worse) · the
hold (rooftop) · no wink · the lonely thing underneath is the whole engine.
