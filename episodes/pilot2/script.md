# pilot "No Evidence" (v2, after critics)
True thing: everyone is in everyone's photos, and nobody has their own.
PXI engine: face match + shared album (by their absence) · Want: proof the night happened, and the girl · Flaw: he'd rather run a procedure than ask anyone a normal question · Cost: $65, a day, and his dignity for an elbow
After watching, a viewer would: think of the photos of themselves sitting on other people's phones.
Sets: Room, Rooftop, Exterior (lobby + stairwell board inside the front door) · New: lobby table, sign, flyer, glow-stick

Critic fixes (Skeptic 3/5, Nine-Year-Old 2/5): hook was slow → open on his lit face and the empty roll in 2 s;
middle was three doors of the same gag → it escalates into a paid photo-return desk in the lobby (procedure, money,
a queue of polite neighbours); the rooftop hold is shortened to one held shot; every beat reads with the sound off.

## Script
(BEDROOM, MORNING. CU: Nio on the floor in last night's clothes, a glow-stick still lit on his wrist, his face lit by his phone. Grinning.)
NIO: Best night of my life.
INSERT: Photos. The newest photo: an empty apartment, boxes. "Oct 21". Nothing after it.
(The grin drops.)
NIO: (to the lens) No evidence.

(MEMORY: ROOFTOP, LAST NIGHT. Warm, soft, music. A girl in a green varsity jacket laughing so hard she bends over.)
MAYA: (memory) You said "actually" again!
(Forty phones up, flashing, all pointed at the two of them.)
(She types her number into his phone. The screen goes black in her hand. A dead-battery icon.)
MAYA: (memory, shouting over the music) I'll send you the pics!
(The crowd swallows her.)

(KITCHEN WALL, DAY. A sheet of paper, his handwriting, numbered.)
INSERT: 1. GREEN JACKET 2. LAUGHS AT "ACTUALLY" 3. ~40 PHONES 4. EST. 212 PHOTOS
NIO: Somewhere there are two hundred photos of me having friends.

(DOOR 3A. A neighbour in a towel holds out his phone, patient.)
INSERT: the neighbour's selfie. Behind him, a blur that might be Nio.
NIO: Can I have it?

(DOOR 4C. A woman shows him her story without looking up.)
INSERT: her drink. At the edge of the frame, an elbow.
NIO: That's my elbow.
WOMAN: Okay.

(THE LOBBY, LATER. Nio behind a folding table, a bowl of mints, a sign in his handwriting:)
INSERT: PHOTO RETURNS · $5 A PHOTO · $20 IF MY FACE IS IN IT
(A polite queue of neighbours. He pays each one in cash.)
INSERT: a ceiling. (He pays $5.)
INSERT: the moon. (He pays $5.)
(A guy reaches the front and lights up.)
GUY: You're the "actually" guy!
INSERT: the back of Nio's head.
NIO: Is my face in it?
GUY: It's the back of it.
(A long look. Nio slides him a twenty.)

(ROOFTOP, NIGHT. Empty. The party's string lights are still up. Nio sits where they danced, scrolling: a blur, an elbow, the back of his head. $65. Hold.)

(STAIRWELL. Going down, he passes his flyer on the board. Stops. Backs up.)
INSERT: his flyer, DID YOU TAKE A PHOTO OF ME? Under his face, in green pen: "I have 61. Same roof, Saturday. — green jacket"
(He reads it twice. No number. No name.)

(HIS ROOM. He opens his laptop to a blank page and types.)
INSERT: every photo you're in, from everyone's phones.
NIO: (to the lens) Okay.
NIO: Never again.
