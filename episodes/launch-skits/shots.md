# Launch skits: shot notes and intent ledger

Storyboards and final lines: project files `nio-soul/skits/launch-skits.html` (writers' room). Shot lists in code:
`studio/anim/src/ep/skits/` (oneSip.ts, groupPhoto.ts, aux.ts, waveBack.ts). Voice medium per skit: experiment E6
