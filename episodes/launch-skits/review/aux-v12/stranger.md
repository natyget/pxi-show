# Stranger review: aux-v12

1. What is this? A cartoon of a house party where a guy grabs the aux, plays songs from a music app, and the room starts raining, then flips so everyone is on the ceiling, and nobody quite says why.

2. Does it make sense? Partly. I think a guy (brown curly hair, purple top) wants the aux, a friend says "Just don't make it worse", and the phone's music app then controls the room: "Rainy Day Piano" gives rain, "Through The Roof" puts people on the ceiling. That cause is shown on the phone screens and it works. First moment I was lost: 0.2s, "That girl's reading the fire exit sign." I can't tell who says it, who "that girl" is, or why it matters, and the exit sign I see at 3.9s is just a blank green rectangle. It is never a setup for anything later. I was lost again at 37.3s, "Bro, film my feet." Who says it and why? At 60.0s, "Thank you." Who thanks whom, for what? The end is unclear. Two people on a porch at night, "Same time next weekend?", then "He's not worth it." / "Worth it." I can't say who speaks either line or who "he" is. Speakers are mostly off-screen or seen from behind, so I could not assign most lines to anyone.

3. Over-explained? "Okay, okay, I'm stopping it." and "Nio! Turn it off!" / "I'm trying!" say what the picture already shows. "Next weekend?" (61.5s) then "Same time next weekend?" (70.1s) says the same thing twice. "My shoe's still up there" explains a gag I would have got from the shoe.

4. Machine or advert? "NOW PLAYING" is a label on the phone screens, fine as an app, but the app is a stock music-player look and the song titles "Rainy Day Piano" and "Through The Roof" are on-the-nose literal. "He's not worth it." / "Worth it." reads as a written punchline, not something friends say. The end lock screen ("Theo's House Party / lena.p posted 4 photos / jaz.k posted a photo / dre_m and 31 others posted to the album", Sunday, November 23, 1:13) is a lot of text with no reason I can see; I looked at the big clock first and the notifications are there for no reason I can tell. On the music screens I looked at the cover art first; the progress bar and buttons are filler.

5. Same person? No. Compared with the golden frames: faces are missing or blank in several shots. At 10.6s (looks like Theo, orange top) it is a bare skin-coloured head with no eyes; at 50.4s the dark-haired friend (Sami) has a smooth blank face with no eyes or nose; at 60.4s the same character has no eyes while the one next to him has them; at 7.2s the curly-haired character's face is blank. Hair in close-ups (7.2s Nio, 10.6s Theo) is much bigger and chunkier than the golden frames. At 27.2s the eyes are huge and different from the golden eyes (heavier lids and outlines). Sami's yellow vest and Theo's red top are there, but the party hats and rain overlay make several people hard to match. Maya's earrings and bun are not clearly seen in any shot.

6. Looks wrong or cheap? Yes. Faces with no features (50.4s, 60.4s, 10.6s). The 23.9s shot is a wall of bookshelves with nothing in it, a stretch of nothing. The ceiling shots (37.1s, 40.5s) are upside-down people against a floor that is also a ceiling, hard to read. The bubbles on the 43.8s phone are floating with no explanation. The rain overlay looks like a flat texture sheet. Blocky pixel edges on the big close-ups look blurry and rough.

7. Stop scrolling for the cover? No. It is a tiny crowd seen from behind in a room, no face, no text, no focal point, and a faint rain line. From the cover alone I would think it is a crowded library or lobby. I would not guess a party or a joke.

8. Watch to the end / send it? Not sure I would reach the end; the middle is confusing and the ending is flat. I would not send it to anyone.

9. Laugh or smile? Mild smile at the people stuck on the ceiling at 37s. Nothing else.

10. Anyone alive? Mostly no. Many shots are the back of someone's head (3.9s, 7.2s, 10.6s), so I see no faces acting. At 0.6s a crowd stands staring with no clear action. Blank-faced characters (50.4s, 60.4s) can't act at all. The porch scene (63.7s to 67.0s) is a long static wide shot with two people sitting still; nothing seems to happen for several seconds (about 62-70s, only dings on the audio). The 23.9s bookshelf shot is a wall. In the 27.2s shot the character stares straight at the camera with huge eyes while the line is something else, so he looks at me, not at anyone in the story. 

11. Explaining a plan or selling something? "Okay, okay, I'm stopping it." and the song-title phone screens feel like a demo of an app feature. The final lock-screen notifications read as a product shot of an app showing off photo sharing.

12. Sound vs picture? From audio.txt: 0-12s speech and music, fits. 12-16s "Ding" at the music-card, ok. 20-28s rain plus speech fits the indoor rain. 32-36s music only, no speech, fine. 36-40s "Howl 0.15, Baby cry 0.11" and 40-44s "Meow 0.10" are not in the scene (low scores, but they are there). 28-32s "Explosion 0.17". 64-72s the night porch is heard as "Ding / Sound effect / Beep / Clang" with almost no speech and no night ambience, so the quiet porch sounds like notification pings, not a night scene. 72-76s "Groan, Sigh". Several stretches do not match the picture.

**FAIL**. Most important fix: the faces. Several shots show characters with no eyes or features and several line speakers are never seen, so I can't tell who is talking or acting; fix the missing faces and put the speaker on screen.

VERDICT: FAIL
