# nopics "No Pics"
Type: Standalone · Tone: Funny
Kind: a small thing that balloons
From life: the founder's own example (Nov 11): the best night of his life, and not one photo of him from it.
Logline: Nio wants one photo of himself from the best night of his life, but every photo in the group chat has only his thumb in it, and he responds by re-staging the whole night alone in his room on a ten-second timer.
Nio's game: with Maya on speaker. The game is making her admit the group cropped him out on purpose. It stops being a game when the push lands and the album has photos of him from the night that he never knew were taken.
Retell: "He's in zero pics from the best night ever so he redoes the whole party alone in his room on self-timer and it's all going live into the group album."

## Beats
Ordinary moment: morning, Nio on the bed edge in last night's shirt, happily scrolling the group chat's photos.
Itch: every photo is great and has his thumb in the corner. He is in none of them.
Theory: to Maya on speaker, calmly: they cut him out, it is jealousy, Sami lost at pool.
So 1: so he zooms for proof, and finds himself two pixels big in a stranger's sunglasses. Evidence.
So 2: so Maya tells him the truth kindly (he held the phone all night), so he decides she is in on it.
So 3: so he will make his own: phone on the dresser, PXI Camera, ten-second timer, the lamp aimed at the rug.
Tilt: alone, he becomes a nightclub. Full dance, full commitment, for a phone.
Set piece: flash after flash, and on Maya's end the group chat starts going off.

Out: Maya, carefully: why is he dancing in the group album. The phone flashes once more. Cut.

## Script
[INT. NIO'S ROOM, MORNING. Nio on the edge of the bed in last night's shirt, phone up, thumb flying. Lit up.]
NIO (delighted, to nobody): Look at Sami. Look at this man on a table.
INSERT: the group chat "friday". Sami on a table. The girls by the door. Everyone. Everyone. In the corner of every one, a blurry thumb.
[His smile drains, one photo at a time. He holds his own thumb up next to the screen. It matches.]
[He calls. Maya on speaker, the phone face up on his knee.]
MAYA (on speaker, bright, mouth full): Morning! Wasn't it the best?
NIO (very calm): How many photos am I in.
MAYA: Loads!
NIO: Name one.
[A pause on the phone. She is honestly thinking.]
MAYA (sincere): The one with your elbow.
NIO (leaking a laugh, honestly asking): Describe the elbow.
MAYA (trying her best): It looked... happy?
[Nio shuts his eyes. Then, smug, a theory he likes.]
NIO: They cut me out. Sami's been weird since I beat him at pool.
MAYA (kind): Nio, you had the phone all night. You kept going "one more, chin up."
NIO (instantly, suspicious): Sami got to you.
MAYA: He's asleep on my floor.
[He zooms into a photo. Zooms. Zooms. In a stranger's sunglasses, two pixels: Nio, mid-shout.]
NIO (hyped, holding it up to the empty room): There he is. Evidence.
MAYA: That's a sunglass.
[Hard cut. The phone propped on the dresser. INSERT: PXI Camera, shooting into the album "friday", timer 10. Nio backs onto the rug and swings the desk lamp onto himself.]
MAYA (on speaker, from across the room): What's that beeping?
NIO (calm, to the dresser): Nothing. Getting ready.
[The music comes in. Alone, he becomes a nightclub: full dance, pointing at nobody, the face of a man at his own party. Flash. Flash. Each one lands in "friday" as a new photo.]
MAYA (slowly): Nio. The group chat is going off.
[Mid-spin, a push lands on the propped phone. He walks over, still half dancing.]
INSERT: "You might be in 38 photos"
[He taps it: the photos his friends took of him last night. Him laughing, head back. Him carrying someone's shoes at a door. Him asleep against a cab window, a jacket over him. The music is still going. He has stopped. A small, real smile.]
MAYA (on speaker, careful): Why are you dancing in the group album?
[Nio, frozen, one arm still up. The phone flashes once more. Cut.]
