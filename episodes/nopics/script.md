Itch: every photo is great and has his thumb in the corner. He is in none of them.
Theory: to Maya on speaker, calmly: they cut him out, it is jealousy, Sami lost at pool.
So 1: so he zooms for proof, and finds himself two pixels big in a stranger's sunglasses. Evidence.

## Script
[INT. NIO'S ROOM, THE NEXT EVENING. Rain on the window. The party was here last night: a paper cup on the dresser. Nio on the edge of the bed, phone up, thumb flying. Lit up.]
NIO (delighted, to nobody): Look at Sami. Look at this man on my desk.
INSERT: his camera roll. This room last night, dark and full: Sami on the desk, arms up. The girls by the door. Everyone. In the corner of every one, a blurry thumb.
[His smile drains, one photo at a time. He holds his own thumb up next to the screen. It matches.]
[He calls. Maya on speaker, the phone face up on his knee.]
MAYA (on speaker, bright, mouth full): Hiii! Wasn't last night the best?
NIO (very calm): How many photos am I in.
MAYA: Loads!
NIO: Name one.
[A pause on the phone. She is honestly thinking.]
MAYA (sincere): The one with your elbow.
NIO (leaking a laugh, honestly asking): Describe the elbow.
MAYA (trying her best): It looked... happy?
[Nio shuts his eyes. Then, smug, a theory he likes.]
NIO: They cut me out. Sami's been weird since I beat him at pool.
MAYA (kind): Nio, you had the phone all night. You kept going "one more, chin up."
NIO (instantly, suspicious): Sami got to you.
MAYA: He's asleep on my floor.
[He zooms into a photo. Zooms. Zooms. In a stranger's sunglasses, two pixels: Nio, mid-shout.]
NIO (hyped, holding it up to the empty room): There he is. Evidence.
MAYA: That's a sunglass.
[Hard cut. INSERT: PXI Camera, flipped to selfie, the live album pill: FRIDAY. Nio steps onto the rug and holds the phone out at arm's length.]
MAYA (on speaker): What's that clicking?
NIO (calm, a boast): Round two.
[The music comes in. Alone, he becomes a nightclub: full dance, pointing at nobody, the face of a man at his own party. Click. Click. Each one lands in "friday" as a new photo.]
MAYA (slowly): Nio. The group chat is going off.
[Mid-spin, a push lands on the phone in his hand. He looks, still half dancing.]
INSERT: "You might be in 38 photos"
[He taps it: the photos his friends took of him last night. Him laughing so hard his eyes are shut. Him mid-shout, from above. Him asleep sitting up on this bed, the party still going around him. The music is still going. He has stopped. A small, real smile.]
MAYA (on speaker, careful): Why are you dancing in the group album?
[Nio, frozen, one arm still up. The phone clicks once more. Cut.]
