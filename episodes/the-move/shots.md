# The Move: board

Script: `episodes/the-move/script.md` (season 1, episode 1; lint passes). 24 fps, 9:16. Short cut **about 46 s**
(S shots), long cut about 50 s (S and L). The season plan lists The Move as a short only, so the L shots are an
option, not a second release.

**Where people are (the club floor, seen from the DJ booth side).** Nio in the middle of a cleared circle. Theo
front left of the circle, phone up and filming. Maya in the circle, right of Theo, dancing small, a cup in her
left hand. The GIRL (a stranger, own design from the crowd generator, kept the same all episode) right of Nio,
her two FRIENDS behind her. The DJ in a raised booth behind Nio, seen in the wides. Two hundred people around
them (the crowd generator: every one different, none blocks or blur except in Nio's point of view).

**In hands.** Theo: his phone, the whole scene. Maya: a cup, which she puts down on the floor in 14. Nio:
nothing at the club; his phone and sunglasses in the morning. The lens: on Nio's left shoulder from shot 4 on,
a small bright dot, visible in 9 and 13 if you look, never pointed at until Maya finds it.

**Faces** use the rig's named presets (`studio/anim/src/nw3d/face.ts`); a name in quotes is a mix described in
the row.

Short cut: shots 1-21, 1101 frames, about 46 s. Long cut: 22 goes in before 19, and 23 before 21, about 50 s.

**PROOF (the hardest seconds to make play): shots 8 to 10, 15.0-22.1 s.** The crowd copying a move, the
high wide of two hundred different people sweeping in time, and the floor-level close-up against a blurred
crowd. If the crowd cannot do this, the episode does not work.

**The picture joke** is shot 9 (12 frames of thud and nothing else first). **The holds** are 4 (26 f), 9
(12 f) and 16 (14 f). **The smash cut** and **the place the music stops dead** are both 18.

## Changes from the script, and why

- **"Theo took it. It's in my album." is long-cut only.** The insert in 19 already shows it is Theo's photo
  in his album, and the short cut needs the second. The script's last line is unchanged.
- **Maya is placed in the circle from shot 1**, so her arrival in 14 is not from nowhere.
- **The lens is visible on his shoulder in 11** for anyone who looks, so "It was on you" is fair.

## Voices

Nio and Maya: the approved designed voices (`bible/characters.md`). **Theo, the GIRL and the CLUB have no
approved voice.** The lines and acting notes are in `studio/voice/jobs_the-move.json`. Nothing is generated: the
voice model cannot be downloaded in this environment (huggingface.co is blocked), and the three new voices need
the founder's ear before they go in a cut.

## Phone screen

Shot 19 (and 22): the PXI shared album, from `app/album/[id].tsx` (header: back arrow, the album name in
upper case, 900 weight; gallery as `AlbumMasonryGrid`, three columns of square tiles) as already matched in
`studio/anim/src/ui/PilotScreens.tsx`. The album name is lower case in the script ("theo's 22nd 🪩"); the header
shows it in upper case as the app does: "THEO'S 22ND 🪩". The photo in the top tile is rendered from the
episode's own club set with the PXI flash look.

## Pacing check

Every shot is 1.2 s or longer; speech is at most 2.3 words a second (shots 2, 3 and 21 sit at the
ceiling); the album insert is held 2.5 s; no on-screen text other than the phone; no standing label; subtitles
carry every line so it reads with the sound off.
