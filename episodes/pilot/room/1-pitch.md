# Pilot rewrite · Stage 1: Pitch

## Why the last pilot didn't work (founder note, Nov 9, evening)
"Nonsense that isn't funny. It's trying to be absurdism and funny but instead corny and lame." The "One Photo" draft
(Nio lies on the floor, a stall owner holds a lamp, the sock guy appears with a gold reflector, a pigeon photobombs,
Nio climbs a roof and it collapses) has the same problem: the weird things are *added* to the scene. Nobody in it
wants anything, so nothing is caused, so nothing is funny. It's a list of props.

## What we're doing instead
Nathan Fielder / Gumball / Regular Show / Clarence all start in a real, small social problem, and the escalation is
built only from the lead's character: his ego, his inability to say one hard sentence, his confident bad plan. The
world stays normal. Other people are nice, which is worse. The laugh comes from the gap between what Nio should say
and what he says, and from silences held one beat too long. Big things can happen, but only as the logical result of
the last small thing.

Fielder rules we borrow (the discipline, never his formats or bits):
- A real goal anyone has had (get a bad photo deleted). Small stakes, treated seriously.
- The lead can't say the plain sentence, so he takes a long, committed route around it.
- Politeness is the trap: every kind reply from a stranger makes it harder to back out.
- Silence is a set piece. Hold the awkward frame longer than is comfortable, then cut.
- Nobody winks. Nobody says anything funny on purpose.

## Six loglines (thrown away first: "One Photo, but grounded" and "Nio rehearses asking Maya something")
1. **Not Me.** Face match shows Nio, mid-bite with sauce on his chin, in the back of a stranger family's "first night
   in the city" album; he walks over to ask the dad to delete it and can't say the word "delete".
   *Logic: politeness spiral; the dismissal engine is the escalation.*
2. **The Knee.** Before his first dinner with Maya's friends, Nio studies her Shared Album to learn their names, and
   arrives knowing far too much. *Logic: over-preparation reads as creepy.*
3. **Wrong Wave.** He waves back at someone who wasn't waving at him and commits to pretending he knew the person
   behind them. *Logic: covering a tiny mistake with a bigger one.* (Too close to a photo already in Ep 1.)
4. **Read Receipt.** Maya's Thread shows "Seen 11:02". He drafts a reply for an hour. *Logic: overthinking.* (Hard to
   make visual; all phone.)
5. **The Plus-One.** Maya adds Nio to a group plan as her "+1" and he reads it as a date. *Logic: misreading signals.*
   (Romance pressure on a pilot; parked.)
6. **Night Wrap.** His Night Wrap scrapbook is three photos of the same bus stop. He goes back out at 11:40 PM to
   make his night "look like a night". *Logic: performing your life for an app.* (Good. Second pick.)

## Premise-machine test
#1 throws jokes without wordplay: every kind thing the family does is a new trap (they think he's offering to take
their photo; they want one with him; they think he's a local; they invite him to brunch). It keeps going on its own.
#6 is the runner-up.

**APPROVED (proposed): #1, Not Me.** PXI features as the engine, used the way a person would: face match ("You might
be in a photo"), face review (IS THIS YOU? · Yes / Not me), a Shared Album, and a group plan invite. Set: the night
market (already built).
