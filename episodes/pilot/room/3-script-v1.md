# Pilot "Not Me" · Stage 3: Script v1

Punchlines first, setups built backwards. Takes marked **[TAKE]**. Runner: the sauce on his chin.

**1. NIGHT MARKET, 11:12 PM.** Crowd noise, sizzling grill, a string of lights swinging. Nio, two-handed, takes an
enormous bite of a skewer. Maya next to him with a paper boat of dumplings.
*Buzz.* He reads his phone mid-chew.

INSERT, Nio's phone: push, *You might be in a photo · Tap to review*. Tap. **IS THIS YOU?** A dad, a mom, a kid
with a balloon, smiling in front of a lantern stall. Behind them, out of focus but unmistakable: Nio, mid-bite, eyes
half shut, sauce on his chin. Under it: *in "Our first night in the city 🥹" · Dev*. Buttons: **Yes** / **Not me**.

**[TAKE]** Nio, still chewing. Stops chewing.
His thumb taps **Not me**. The screen says *Got it. We won't show you this again.* The photo is still on the screen
behind the toast for a second, then slides away. It still exists.

**2. MS, the two of them.** Nio looks up. Across the aisle, at the next stall: the same family. The dad (Dev)
holding the phone, scrolling, smiling at it.

MAYA *(eating, not looking up)*: Just ask him to delete it.
NIO: Easy. I'm great with dads.
*(He wipes his chin with the back of his hand. The wrong side. The sauce stays.)*

**3. THE FAMILY'S STALL, two-shot.** Nio arrives with his phone held out, screen first, ready to show the evidence.
NIO: Hey, sorry, quick thing—
DEV *(lighting up, already handing over his own phone)*: Oh, would you? Thank you!
*(The family snaps into a pose. The mom fixes the kid's collar. They smile at Nio. They wait.)*
**[TAKE]** Nio, holding two phones. Hold it.
*Shutter.*

**4. INSERT, Dev's phone in Nio's hand.** The new photo: the family, perfect. His thumb swipes once. The old photo.
Him, mid-bite. The trash can icon at the bottom. His thumb moves toward it, slowly.

**CU, the kid.** Holding the balloon. Staring straight at Nio. Doesn't blink.

**CU, Nio.** His thumb stops.
DEV *(off)*: Get a good one?
**[TAKE]** Hold Nio for a full second of nothing. The market noise drops a little.
NIO: ...You have a beautiful family.
DEV *(hand on his heart, genuinely moved)*: Man.

**5. WIDE.** The mom is already pulling Nio into the middle. Maya appears and holds out her hand for Dev's phone,
helpful.
DEV: Where you from?
NIO: Here.
DEV *(to his wife, delighted)*: A local!
MAYA *(raising the phone)*: He knows everything.
*Shutter. Shutter. Shutter.* In each flash Nio's face is the same: a polite smile with sauce on the chin.

**6. MS, Nio and Maya walking back.** *Buzz.* Insert: *Dev added you to "Our first night in the city 🥹".*
*Buzz.* Insert, group plan card: **Brunch with our local guide** · Sun 10:00 AM · *Going: Dev, Priya, Maya*.
**[TAKE]** Nio turns his head to Maya. Maya, chewing, keeps looking at her dumplings.

**7. INSERT, the album.** The grid is full: every new photo has Nio in it with the sauce. A small toast: *Dev changed
the album cover.* The cover is the mid-bite photo. Caption under it: *best one ❤️*.
*Cut to black.*

---
**Spoken words: 46.** Runtime estimate: speech ≈20 s + 13 action beats ≈32 s + takes ≈6 s = **≈58 s** (target 55, +5%).

Notes
- Nio's spoken lines: 5. He never says "delete". The line he should say is cut off once ("quick thing—"), and then
  he never tries again.
- Maya has three lines and does the most damage with the fewest words.
- The group plan says "Going: Maya" without Nio. He's the guest of honour and the only one who hasn't said yes.
