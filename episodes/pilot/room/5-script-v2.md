## Script v2

**1. NIGHT MARKET, 11:12 PM.** Grill sizzle, crowd, a string of lights swinging in the wind. Nio takes an enormous
bite of a skewer. Maya beside him with a paper boat of dumplings. His phone buzzes; he reads it mid-chew.

INSERT, his phone: *You might be in a photo.* Tap. **IS THIS YOU?** A family smiling in front of a lantern stall,
and behind them Nio mid-bite, eyes half shut, sauce on his chin. *In "Our first night in the city 🥹".*
His thumb hits **Not me**. The next photo slides in: same moment, a step closer. **Not me.** Closer still, his face
filling half the frame. His thumb hovers.

CU Nio, chewing slowly. He looks up.

**2. HIS POV.** Across the aisle at the next stall: the same family. The dad (Dev) has his phone out, swiping through
the photos, smiling.

**MS, Nio and Maya.**
MAYA *(not looking up from her dumplings)*: Just ask him to delete it.
*(Nio hands her his skewer without looking at her and walks off.)*

**3. THE FAMILY'S STALL.** Nio stops in front of Dev, holding his phone out with the photo on it.
NIO: Hey, sorry—
DEV *(sees a guy holding out a phone, lights up)*: Oh, you want one?
*(Dev holds out his own phone. The mom fixes the kid's collar. They wait.)*
**[TAKE]** Nio looks at Dev's phone. Dev keeps holding it out. The hold goes on a beat too long.
NIO: ...Sure.
*Shutter.*

**4. INSERT, Dev's phone in Nio's hand.** The photo he just took. His thumb swipes back once: the bite. The trash
can at the bottom of the screen. His thumb drifts toward it.

**CU, the kid**, holding a balloon, right next to him.
KID *(flat)*: That's my dad's phone.

**CU, Nio.** The thumb stops.
DEV *(off)*: Get a good one?
**[TAKE]** One full second. Market noise ducks.
NIO: You have a beautiful family.
DEV *(hand on chest)*: Man.

**5. WIDE.** The mom is already pulling Nio into the middle. Dev hands his phone to Maya, who has wandered over with
two skewers.
DEV: Where you from?
NIO: Here.
DEV *(to the mom)*: A local! *(to Maya)* Is he free Sunday?
MAYA *(mouth full)*: Mm-hm.
*Shutter. Flash.*

**6. MS, Nio and Maya walking back.** Buzz. FULL-SCREEN INSERT, plan card:
**Sunday brunch with our local 🧭** · *Going: Dev, Priya, Maya*.
**[TAKE]** Nio turns his head to Maya. Maya eats his skewer.

**7. INSERT, the album.** The cover photo flips to the bite. The title retypes itself, letter by letter:
*Our first ~~night~~ friend in the city 🥹*.
*Cut to black.*
