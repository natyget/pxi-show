# Pilot rewrite · Stage 2: Beat sheet · "Not Me" (~55 s)

Premise: Nio finds out he's mid-bite in a stranger family's photo and tries to get it deleted without ever asking.

| # | Time | Beat | Want / obstacle / turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 s | **Hook.** Night market, loud. Nio takes a huge bite of a skewer. Buzz. Insert: *You might be in a photo.* → **IS THIS YOU?** A family of three smiling, and behind them Nio mid-bite, eyes half shut, sauce on his chin. Album: "Our first night in the city 🥹". | Want: not be that guy. Obstacle: it's in their album. Turn: he taps **Not me**. The photo stays exactly where it is. |
| 2 | 4–11 s | He looks up. The same family is at the next stall, the dad holding the phone. Maya, eating: "Just ask him to delete it." Nio: "Easy. I'm great with dads." He wipes his chin. Wrong side. | Want: a smooth ask. Obstacle: he's never actually great with dads. Turn: he walks over holding his phone out. |
| 3 | 11–20 s | The dad sees a guy holding out a phone and smiles: "Oh, would you? Thank you!" and hands Nio *his* phone. The family poses. Nio holds it. Silence. He takes their photo. | Want: ask. Obstacle: he's been given a job. Turn: he does the job. |
| 4 | 20–31 s | Now he's holding the dad's phone, and the bad photo is one swipe away. He swipes. There it is. His thumb over the trash can. The kid is watching him. Dad: "Get a good one?" Long hold. Nio (pivot instead of asking): "You have a beautiful family." Dad, moved, hand on heart. | Want: delete it himself. Obstacle: a child is watching him go through a stranger's photos. Turn: he compliments them, which makes them love him. |
| 5 | 31–42 s | "Get in one with us!" The mom pulls him in. Maya steps up and takes it (she always helps with the worse plan). Dad: "Where are you from?" Nio: "Here." Dad: "A local!" The family looks at him like he's a gift. | Want: leave. Obstacle: he's now their local. Turn: Maya says "He knows everything." |
| 6 | 42–52 s | Back with Maya. Buzz, buzz. Inserts: *Dev added you to "Our first night in the city 🥹".* Then a group plan: *Brunch with Nio, our local guide · Sun 10 AM · Going: Maya.* Nio looks at Maya. Maya, chewing, doesn't look up. | The cost: he's giving a tour on Sunday. |
| 7 | 52–56 s | **Button.** Insert: the album. The dad changed the cover photo. It's the mid-bite one. Caption: "best one ❤️". Cut. | The photo he wanted gone is now the cover. |

## Runner
Sauce on his chin: in the photo (beat 1) → he wipes the wrong side (2) → still there in every new photo (5, seen on
the album grid) → it's the cover (7).

## What makes it Nio
- Confidence first ("I'm great with dads"), then he can't say one plain word.
- The dismissal engine *is* the plot: every time he should say "delete", he pivots to something sweeter, and each
  pivot makes it worse.
- Maya's chaos is quiet: she signs them up for brunch and keeps eating.

## The weird character
The kid. Seven years old, holding a balloon, never blinks, watches Nio the whole time, says nothing. Nobody else
notices.

## Mundane explanation Nio missed
The family is new to the city and asked the market's group chat for "a local who knows the good spots". (Payoff later.)
