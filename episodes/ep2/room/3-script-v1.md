# Ep 2 "Forty-Seven", script v1 (≈55 s)

**Logline:** Maya has 47 stamps in her PXI passport. Nio has 1. At 11 PM he decides to get 47 by midnight.

## Beats
1. **Hook (0–3 s).** Phone insert: Maya's passport counter rolls up to 47 with a chime. Cut to Nio's profile: **1**.
   The stamp is the Laundromat.
2. **The challenge (3–14 s).** Nio's apartment, 11:02 PM. Maya sits on his bed eating cereal out of his box. Nio
   commits to the scheme. The map is full of pins.
3. **The montage (14–34 s).** He goes to every event at ludicrous speed, and the counter climbs. Maya trails behind him.
   Each event is shorter than the last: a candle sniff at the night market, one dance move on the roof, one sock at the
   laundromat sock swap. Then flash cuts with accelerating stamp sounds, 4 → 46.
4. **The last pin (34–44 s).** 11:59. One pin left nearby, and it's his own building: "Welcome Nio · 9:00 PM · hosted by
   Maya". She's been trying to get him home all night.
5. **The cost (44–55 s).** His apartment: the party is over. Streamers, half a cake, and the sock guy asleep in a chair
   holding a sock. Midnight. STAMP: 47.
6. **Button.** Maya's counter ticks to 48, for hosting. Nio looks at the lens. Cut.

## Dialogue
COLD OPEN, insert. Chime. *47.* Cut: *1. Laundromat.*

NIO (ECU, a take, then): Forty-seven?
MAYA (on his bed, mouth full of his cereal): I go to stuff.
NIO: I go to stuff.
MAYA: You go to the laundromat.
*(Take: Nio. The dryer-shaped silence.)*
NIO (standing, chin up): I'm getting forty-seven. Tonight.
MAYA: It's eleven.
NIO: Then I'll go fast.

INSERT: the PXI map. Pins everywhere. He grins.

MONTAGE
- Night market. Nio sniffs one candle. STAMP 2. He's gone. Maya arrives holding the candle he paid for.
- Rooftop silent disco. Nio does one dance move. STAMP 3. Gone. The DJ keeps looking at the space where he was.
- Laundromat sock swap. The sock guy holds out a sock. Nio takes it. STAMP 4. Gone.
- MAYA (panting, at a door): Was that the whole pottery class?
- Flash cuts, faster: STAMP 9, 15, 22, 31, 38, 46.
- MAYA (somewhere behind him): Can we go back to yours?
- NIO (not stopping): No time.

11:59. Street corner. Nio, breathless, holds the phone up.
INSERT: one pin left nearby. His building. *Welcome Nio · 9:00 PM · hosted by Maya*
*(Take: Nio looks at Maya. Maya looks at her shoes.)*
MAYA (small): I tried to say. At the sock thing.

APARTMENT, 12:00. The door swings open. Streamers. Half a cake. The sock guy asleep in a chair.
STAMP. 47. Chime.
NIO (very quiet): Forty-seven.
*(The sock guy, eyes still closed, holds up a sock.)*
INSERT: Maya's counter ticks: 48. *Host.*
*(Nio looks at the lens. Hold. Cut.)*

**Spoken words: ≈62. Runtime estimate: ≈52–56 s with the montage and takes.**
