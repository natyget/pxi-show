# Ep 2 "Forty-Seven", script v2 (after critique + punch-up + de-AI pass)

**Logline:** Maya has 47 stamps in her PXI passport. Nio has one, from the laundromat. He decides to get 47 before his
dryer finishes.

**What changed from v1 and why:**
- **The logic now holds.** It opens at the laundromat, his only stamp. The party is a midnight surprise on his
  building's roof, so there's no room contradiction and no party he "missed".
- **Maya's silence makes sense.** She was sent to keep him out until midnight. He keeps himself out. We show it with a
  text on her phone, not a line.
- **The twist isn't telegraphed.** "Can we go back to yours?" and "No time." are cut.
- **Nio gets a real Gumball pitch:** one run-on line of confident nonsense. Line lengths now range from 1 word to 30.
- **No echoes, no lens look, one button.** The sock is a runner that comes back three times and reverses at the end.
  Maya's "48 · Host" is cut, because it explained the joke.

## Script

**COLD OPEN.** Phone insert. Maya's passport rolls up: 44… 45… 46… 47. Chime. Cut: Nio's passport. **1.** The stamp is
a washing machine: *Laundromat*.

**LAUNDROMAT, 11:02 PM.** Nio and Maya on the bench. His dryer spins. In the background, the sock guy watches one sock
go round.

NIO (over her shoulder): How.
MAYA (not looking up, scrolling): I just go to the things.
NIO *(stands; the pitch)*: If you go to one thing a minute, which is easy, because a thing is just a room with people
in it, I can get forty-seven before my dryer's done.
MAYA: Your dryer's on sixty.
NIO: Perfect.
*(The sock guy turns his head, very slowly, toward Nio.)*

INSERT: the PXI map. Pins everywhere, more appearing as it zooms out.

**MONTAGE** *(each stop is a different kind of joke, faster each time; the counter is always on screen)*
- **Right here, the laundromat sock swap.** The sock guy holds out one sock. Nio takes it. STAMP 2. *(Sock, 1st time.)*
- **Night market.** Nio sniffs one candle, already walking away. STAMP 3. Maya arrives and pays the stall owner,
  bowing an apology.
- **Rooftop silent disco.** One dance move. STAMP 4. The DJ keeps staring at the spot where he was.
- **A door with a "Pottery" sign.** Nio walks out with a lopsided bowl.
  MAYA (arriving, out of breath): Was that the whole pottery class?
- **Flash cuts, faster.** STAMP 9, 16, 23, 31, 40. The pile in his arms grows: the candle, the bowl, a medal, a
  goldfish in a bag, the sock on top. *(Sock, 2nd time.)*
- **Insert, 11:58.** Maya, jogging, types into a group chat called *SURPRISE SQUAD*: "still out 👍". Send.

**STREET CORNER, 11:59.** Nio, wrecked, arms full, holds the phone up to his face: **46**. One pin left. It's his own
building, the roof, at 12:00.
NIO: That's my building. *(gasps, delighted)* It's a short one!
*(He runs. Maya runs after him, pocketing her phone.)*

**HIS ROOF, 12:00.** The door bursts open. Twenty people jump up: "SURPRISE!" A "WELCOME, NIO" banner. Nio doesn't
look up from the phone. STAMP **47**. Chime.
NIO *(arms up, everything he's holding flies)*: FORTY-SEVEN!
*(The crowd cheers. They think he means the party. Maya, at the back, claps, relieved.)*

**BUTTON.** The sock guy steps out of the crowd and holds out one sock: the match.
*(Nio takes it. Holds both socks. A long beat.)*
NIO (very quiet, sincere): ...They match.
*CUT.*
