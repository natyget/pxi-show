## Script

**COLD OPEN.** Phone insert. Maya's PXI passport rolls up: 44… 45… 46… 47. Chime. Cut to Nio's passport: **1.** The
stamp is *Sock Swap · Laundromat*.

**LAUNDROMAT, 11:02 PM.** Nio holds one sock and pats the empty dryer for the other. Maya sits on the bench scrolling.
In the background, the sock guy (his downstairs neighbour) folds laundry very slowly.

NIO (reading her phone over her shoulder): How.
MAYA: I just go to the things.
NIO *(the pitch, sock in hand)*: If you go to one thing a minute, which is easy, because a thing is just a room with
people in it, I can get forty-seven before my dryer's done.
MAYA: Your dryer's on sixty.
NIO: Perfect.
*(The sock guy turns his head, very slowly, toward Nio.)*

INSERT: the PXI map. Pins everywhere.

**MONTAGE** *(each stop a different kind of joke; the counter always on screen; his one sock always in his hand)*
- **Night market.** Nio sniffs one candle and walks off holding it. STAMP 2. Maya arrives and pays the stall owner.
- **Rooftop silent disco.** One dance move. STAMP 3. He leaves still wearing the DJ's headphones; the DJ, now with no
  headphones, just stands there.
- **A door with a "Pottery" sign.** Nio comes out with a lopsided bowl.
  MAYA (arriving, out of breath): Was that the whole pottery class?
- **Flash cuts, faster.** STAMP 9, 16, 23, 31, 40. The pile in his arms grows: the candle, headphones, the bowl, a
  medal, a goldfish in a bag, and the one sock on top.
  MAYA (keeping pace): Nio, that's a fish.
  NIO: It's a stamp.

**STREET CORNER, 11:59.** Nio, wrecked, arms full, the phone held up to his face: **46**. One pin left before midnight:
*Roof hang · 12:00*. It's his building.
NIO: That's my building. *(gasps, delighted)* It's a short one!

**HIS ROOF, 12:00.** The door bursts open. The whole building jumps up: "SURPRISE!" A banner: *WELCOME TO THE BUILDING,
NIO*. He doesn't look up from the phone. STAMP **47**. Chime.
NIO *(arms up, everything he's holding flies)*: FORTY-SEVEN!
*(Everyone cheers anyway.)*
NEIGHBOUR (to Maya, under the cheering): How'd you keep him out so late?
MAYA: I didn't.

**BUTTON.** The sock guy steps out of the crowd and holds out one sock. *His* sock. It was in the dryer.
*(Nio looks at the sock in his hand. Then at the sock guy's. A long beat.)*
NIO (very quiet, sincere): ...They match.
*CUT.*
