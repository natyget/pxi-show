# Stage 1: Pitch

Thrown away first (they were the first two thoughts, and too close to Ep 1): "PXI finds him in a photo again";
"he says there's nothing to do while a market is next door".

1. **The Lookalike.** Face match keeps finding Nio in a stranger's album. It's a guy who looks like him and is having a
   much better life. Nio sets out to out-live his own lookalike. *Logic: jealousy of yourself.*
2. **The Stamp Race.** Maya has 47 stamps in her PXI passport this month. Nio has one: the laundromat. At 11 PM he
   decides to get 47 by midnight, one second per event. *Logic: an ego scheme run at absurd speed, and it costs him.*
3. **RSVP Everything.** To look popular, Nio taps "going" on every event. The group plans add him to a knitting
   circle, a funeral and a toddler's birthday, and they all come to find him. *Logic: a social lie with consequences.*
4. **The Darkroom.** His Night Wrap develops into a scrapbook, and every print is his own thumb. He insists it's art
   and tries to sell it at the market. *Logic: rationalising a failure into a business.*
5. **Group Plan.** Nio makes a group plan for his first friend group and invites everyone he's met. He has met three
   people, and one of them is the sock guy. *Logic: confidence vs. the size of his world.*
6. **The Look-back.** "1 year ago today" shows a memory from a night Nio is sure he was home alone. *Logic: eerie
   gaslighting.* (Eerie slot; too close to Ep 1's mystery.)

**Premise-machine test:** #2 throws jokes off every event. Each stop is its own gag with no wordplay: one sniff of a
candle, one dance move, one sock, plus the counter climbing. #3 is close behind.
**APPROVED: #2, The Stamp Race.** PXI's passport stamps and map are the engine, used the way a real person would use
them: go to things, get stamped, see what's on.
