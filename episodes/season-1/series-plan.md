# Nio World, season 1: the plan

Written Nov 13 from the founder's note of that day. It replaces "No Pics" as the first thing we post. The
cast and places are in `bible/world.md`, Nio in `bible/characters.md`, the craft in `bible/gumball-style.md`.

## What the show is

**A college kid who is good at everything except taking life seriously, and the people and nights around
him.** Short, funny, easy to follow from one post to the next. You watch it for Nio and his friends, the way
you follow any account you like. Every story is new and stands up on its own.

The tone is a mix of two things the founder named. From the follow-along cartoon accounts: a relatable
situation, a character reasoning through it out loud like a realist, and an ending that lands fast. From
Gumball: an ego that turns a small thing into a big one, a world that stays calm about it, and faces that do
the work.

## PXI in the show (the founder's rule, Nov 13)

**PXI is the phone app Nio's world runs on, the way a phone brand is just there in a show.** Whenever
someone takes a photo, finds something to do, plans a trip or invites people, it is on PXI, because that is
what they would really use. It is never the subject of an episode.

- **Where it shows up by default:** every party, club night, pickup game, birthday, BBQ, trip, cabin weekend,
  holiday, club or team event, and every photo anyone takes. The event page, the map, the shared album, the
  flash.
- **How:** in the hand, on the screen, for a second, doing its job. Characters talk about the night, never
  about the app.
- **The fourth wall:** about one episode in four, after the story is over, Nio turns to us for one line.
  "This my app. Go download PXI." Never in the middle of a story.
- **The test:** take PXI out. If the episode is still funny and still makes sense, it is placed right.
  If it falls apart, it was the subject, and that is wrong.

## Length and cadence

- **Shorts, 30 to 45 seconds**, for Instagram and TikTok. One story each. Three a week to start.
- **Longs, 2 to 4 minutes**, for YouTube, when a story has room to breathe (marked LONG on the slate).
- No "part 1". A story that needs more room gets a long cut, not a sequel.

## Where it happens (no two episodes in a row in the same place)

Rooftops, clubs, a speakeasy behind a laundromat, a game bar, classrooms and lecture halls, the campus
quad, the five-a-side pitch, the gym, the corner shop, house parties in other people's houses, the lift and
lobby of his building, a cabin, a car, the street at 2 a.m. **His apartment is where stories start and end,
not where parties happen.** Nobody dances alone in a bedroom unless that is the joke.

## The slop list (never write these again)

The founder, Nov 13: "every story needs to be new and not polluted by any inside jokes or lame humor."
These came from earlier drafts and models and keep coming back. Any of them in a script is a defect.

- The elbow. Talk of "evidence", "proof", "pics or it didn't happen", "no photos of me".
- "Who is this", the man in the beanie, the guy who was there the whole time, glitch endings by default.
- The old catchphrases: "Yeah, what's up", "I'm just tired", "They left", "This can't be real".
- Numbers as drama ("40 phones, 0 photos"), labels with dots, "Part 1".
- Fortune cookies, bingo cards, tier lists and other filler formats with no joke in them.
- A party in his bedroom. Nio talking to himself in different places. Nio explaining the app.
- Any line, prop or bit from a previous episode, unless it is a deliberate, named callback.

**Freshness check:** before a script goes forward, the writer lists the last ten scripts' main props, places
and jokes, and nothing in the new one may repeat them.

## What these scripts need from the look and the engine

Passed to the design and engine threads, in the founder's words of Nov 13: every friend in their own design
(hair, head shape, build, clothes, face) and a reusable crowd of background people who belong in the world;
the same hands holding the phone as in every other shot, thumbs included; real mouth shapes for speech; the
PXI flash and filter visible in the world when someone takes a photo, with the photo coming out a little
nicer than life; and the places above, built once and reused.
