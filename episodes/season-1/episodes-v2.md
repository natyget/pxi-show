## 1. Office Hours

Where: a lecture hall, a corridor that loops, a waiting room, a tiny office, the pitch · Chore: go to office
hours · Dodge: Nio decides they are optional · The impossible thing: office hours are a place, and the
people who skipped them are still in it · Skill that saves him: he can read a lonely man and hype him up ·
PXI: the map shows a building that isn't on campus · Eerie: Darnell · About 60 s

**Professor Haverbrook ("Prof H")**, new to the cast: an enormous, soft man in a cardigan with tiny round
glasses who speaks in a whisper the microphone turns into thunder. He is desperate for his students to think
he is cool, learns their slang from the internet and uses it nearly right. Everyone on campus treats his
office hours the way you treat a haunted house. He has no idea.

```
[INT. LECTURE HALL, 4:40 PM. PROF H closes his laptop. His whisper fills the room through the mic.]
PROF H: Great sesh, fam. Real ones come to office hours.
[He looks straight at Nio.]
PROF H: Nio. Today.

[Thirty heads turn. A girl in the next row slowly takes her cap off, like at a funeral.]
THEO (barely moving his lips): You're cooked.
NIO (packing up, easy): It says hours. Plural. That's a suggestion.
THEO: Darnell said that.
NIO: Who's Darnell?
[Theo looks at the seat beside him. Empty. On it: a water bottle, a 2019 planner, and a layer of dust.]
NIO (beat): ...Pickup's at five.

[INT. CORRIDOR. Fluorescent hum. At the far end, a door, wide open, warm light pouring out. The plaque:
"PROF. HAVERBROOK. MY DOOR IS ALWAYS OPEN 🙂". Nio turns the other way. Walks. Turns a corner. The same
door, ahead of him. A little more open.]
NIO: No.
[He takes the stairs down two flights, bursts through the stairwell door: the same corridor, the same door.]
[He opens the PXI map. His blue dot is inside a grey building that is not on campus. Event card:
"Office Hours. Always. 1 going." He is the one.]
NIO (quietly): I did not RSVP to that.
[A CLEANER mops past, unbothered.]
CLEANER: Just go in, baby. It's faster.

[Nio sprints. SMASH CUT: he is sitting in a WAITING ROOM. Plastic chairs. A ticket machine. A clock with no
hands. The music has stopped; only the hum. Other students wait with papers on their knees: a boy with a
fidget spinner, a girl in a flower crown, and DARNELL, beard to his belt, very calm, holding a lab report
with a coffee ring on it. Above the inner door: NOW SERVING 3.]

DARNELL: First time?
NIO: I'm not even here.
DARNELL (nodding, kind): Yeah. We all were.
[Nio looks at the ticket in Darnell's hand. 4.]
DARNELL (gently, like it's nothing): Did they ever drop the new GTA?
[A long beat. Nio cannot do it to him.]
NIO: ...Not yet, bro.
[Darnell nods, satisfied, and goes back to waiting.]

[The door opens. PROF H fills it sideways, delighted.]
PROF H: Nio! Come in, come in.

[INT. OFFICE. Tiny. Prof H folded behind the desk. A HANG IN THERE cat poster. A bobblehead of himself.]
PROF H: So. I wanted a word.
[Hold. Room tone. Nio, sweating.]
PROF H (very small): Do the students think I'm... HTN?
[Nio looks at him. The man's lip is going. Nio leans in, all the way, fully committed.]
NIO: Prof. You're mogging the whole faculty. Everybody says it.
PROF H (a whisper, a tear): ...Mogging.

[Behind Nio, the door swings open onto the sunny quad. In the waiting room the clock has hands. NOW SERVING
4. Darnell stands, squints at the sun, and walks out into it, beard first.]
PROF H: Same time next week?
[Nio is already gone. The door clicks shut by itself.]

[EXT. FIVE-A-SIDE PITCH, 5:01 PM. Nio jogs on, hair wrecked.]
THEO: Where were you?
NIO: Office hours.
[Theo goes white and takes one step back.]
```

Laughs: the cap off like a funeral / the dusty seat / "I did not RSVP to that" / "Just go in, baby. It's
faster." / Darnell's beard / "Did they ever drop the new GTA?" and "Not yet, bro" / "HTN?" / "mogging the
whole faculty" / Theo stepping back.
Eerie: the corridor that loops, the clock with no hands, NOW SERVING 3, Darnell's calm. Never explained.
Caption: skipped office hours one time. Who's your Darnell?

## 2. The Move

Where: a club in the basement under a ramen place, then his couch · Chore: nothing, he is out · Dodge: he
puts a contact back in off the floor · The impossible thing: an infection that happens on the beat · PXI: the
flash photo and the album, and the comments · Eerie: the morning · About 50 s

```
[INT. BASEMENT CLUB, 1 AM. Low ceiling, red lights, a DJ behind two crates. NIO, three drinks deep, is doing
the stupidest dance in the room on purpose: he rotates slowly with his arms out, stops, and goes "beep" (the
microwave). Then he jerks forward in little stops (the printer jam). His friends are crying. THEO is filming.]

SAMI (swaying, formal): He has been a microwave for four minutes.

[Nio spins. His head snaps. Freeze. His point of view: a red smear.]
NIO: Nobody move. I dropped an eye.

[He goes down on his knees, sweeping the sticky floor in slow circles, face an inch from it. Two girls watch,
look at each other, and drop down beside him, sweeping in time. A guy in a bucket hat joins. Four of them.
The DJ, unsure, slows the beat to match.]
GIRL (sweeping, earnest): Is this a trend?
NIO (not looking up): It's a contact.
GIRL (to her friend): It's called "the contact."

[Nio finds it. It is in a little puddle of something blue, between a bottle cap and one single fry. He holds
it up to the light. It glistens.]
MAYA (above him, horrified): Nio. No.
NIO (certain, holding it up): Club floor's basically saline. It's salt and water. That's science.
MAYA: There's a fry.

[He pops it in. Blinks twice. Smiles.]
NIO: Crisp.

[The bass hits. His eyelid puffs up. The bass hits again. Bigger, redder, shiny. On every kick drum it
swells, until it is a throbbing plum keeping perfect time with the track. The four sweepers stand up slowly.
The DJ stops the music. Silence. A fridge hums somewhere.]
NIO (calm, one eye): ...Is it bad?
[Theo raises his phone. PXI flash.]

[CUT TO: NIO'S APARTMENT, MORNING. Grey light. Nio on the couch, frozen peas on half his face. He opens the PXI
album "basement 🍜". At the top, Theo's photo. The PXI filter has made it gorgeous: warm grain, perfect light,
the whole club glowing. In the middle, Nio, one eye like a tomato, giving a thumbs up. Reactions keep ticking.
The comments scroll:]
INSERT: "bro got mogged by the floor 💀"
INSERT: "he said SALINE"
INSERT: "the fry won"
INSERT: "aura -10000"
INSERT: "it's giving pirate who lost the war"
INSERT (Maya): "you look so brave 🥺"

[The good eye fills up. The bad eye tries. A single enormous tear forms on the swollen one, slowly, like honey,
and rolls down his cheek. Room tone. The notifications keep landing, soft, like rain on a window. The light in
the room goes blue. Hold.]
[One more notification. Theo has added a photo: an extreme close-up of the eye. Nio sobs, silently, once.]
[CUT TO BLACK.]
```

Laughs: the microwave / "I dropped an eye" / "It's called 'the contact'" / the fry / "That's science" /
"Crisp" / the eye swelling on the beat / the beautiful photo of the ugly eye / the comments / the close-up.
Caption: put my contact back in off the club floor. 0/10 do not recommend. Rate the eye 🍅

## 3. Lost and Found (was Password)

Where: a laundromat, the bottom of the lost-and-found bin, a speakeasy door · Chore: get into Theo's birthday
· Dodge: they reach into the bin instead of texting Theo · The impossible thing: the lost-and-found is a world
· Skill that saves him: his hands (a cube solved without looking) · PXI: the event page and its joke password
clue · Eerie: the cavern of lost things, and what is lonely down there · Short about 60 s; LONG about 3 min

```
[EXT. LAUNDROMAT, 11 PM. Dryers turning. A door at the back with a slot. NIO, MAYA and SAMI, dressed up. On
Nio's phone, the PXI event page: "Theo's 22nd 🎂 back of Suds. Password's in the lost and found 😈"]
[Knock. The slot opens: two eyes.]
DOORMAN: Password.
NIO: One sec.

[A plastic tub labelled LOST + FOUND in marker. Nio reaches in up to the elbow. Then the shoulder. Then his
head goes in.]
SAMI: How deep is that bin.
NIO (echoing, from inside): ...It's not a bin.
[He falls in. Maya claps once, thrilled, and jumps after him. Sami looks at the bin. Looks at the slot. The
eyes look back at him. Sami jumps.]

[INT. THE LOST AND FOUND. A vast quiet cavern lit like the bottom of a pool. Dunes of single socks. A river
of hair ties. A forest of umbrellas, open, upside down. One low note of music. Far off, something breathes.]
MAYA (whispering, moved): This is where the other socks go.
[Nio picks up an earbud. Then another. Then a whole drift of them.]
NIO: These are mine. These are all mine.
[He pulls out a hoodie, smells it.]
NIO: Tenth grade. It still smells like tenth grade.

[A rumble. Out of a hill of cables rises THE TANGLE: a giant knotted from every wired earphone ever lost,
with two little earbuds for eyes. It is very sad.]
THE TANGLE (deep, slow): Untangle... me...
SAMI: Absolutely not, thank you.

[It lunges. A chase across the sock dunes, played completely straight, music going full epic. Maya stops,
turns, and opens her arms to it.]
MAYA (sincere): Do you want to talk about how you feel?
[The Tangle sobs and knots itself tighter. Maya, apologetic: "That's on me."]

[Nio stops running. Breathes. Climbs the Tangle, finds the end of one wire, half closes his eyes, and starts
working the knots at speed-cuber speed, fingers a blur, totally calm.]
NIO: Over, under, pull. Bro's just a seven-by-seven.
[The final pull. The giant falls apart into a single neat coil of wired earphones in Nio's hand. Two little
earbud eyes look up at him.]
THE TANGLE (tiny now): Thank you. The password is "lint."

[EXT. LAUNDROMAT. The three of them climb out of the bin, covered in socks. Nio knocks. Slot.]
NIO (triumphant): Lint.
DOORMAN: That was last week's.
[Slot shuts.]

[Silence. Sami lies down on a running dryer and closes his eyes. Maya pats a sock off Nio's shoulder. Nio,
beaten, does the only thing he knows how to do. He puts the old wired earphones in his ears.]
[The slot slides open. The eyes see the wire. They widen.]
DOORMAN (with deep respect): ...Wired?
[The door swings open on music and warm light. THEO, inside: "Finally." Nio walks in like a king, wire
swinging. Sami is still on the dryer, going round with it.]
```

LONG cut adds: a montage of the cavern (the umbrella forest, a sock with a name tag, Maya finding a love
letter nobody sent and deciding to deliver it); and Nio finding his first ever phone, which still has 4%.
Laughs: "It's not a bin" / Sami jumping because the eyes are watching / "Tenth grade" / "Absolutely not,
thank you" / Maya's therapy making it worse / "Bro's just a seven-by-seven" / "That was last week's" /
"...Wired?" / Sami on the dryer.
Eerie: the cavern, the breathing, the giant made of lost things that only wants to be undone.
Caption: the password was in the lost and found. We did not expect the lost and found to be that big.
What did you lose that's down there?

## 4. On a Break

Where: a rooftop at golden hour · Chore: hang out, say nothing about the couple · Dodge: Nio asks the one
honest question · The impossible thing: a break has a referee, and he has been here the whole time · PXI: the
rooftop album, used as the video replay · Eerie: the referee's last line · About 55 s

```
[EXT. ROOFTOP, GOLDEN HOUR. Folding chairs, string lights, a cooler. JULES and KAI sit two chairs apart,
not looking at each other. Standing between them, arms folded, in a black-and-white striped shirt, whistle
in his mouth: a REFEREE. Nobody looks at him.]

MAYA (leaning in, delighted, quiet): They're on a break.
NIO (looking straight at the referee): Okay. And who's he?
MAYA: That's the break.
[The referee nods at Nio, professionally.]

[Kai reaches for the chips. Jules reaches for the chips. Their fingers touch.]
[WHISTLE. The referee strides in and holds up a yellow card to both of them.]
KAI: That was the chips!
JULES (to the ref): He knows I like the salt and vinegar ones.
[The referee writes it down.]

NIO (honestly asking): How long is a break?
REFEREE (without looking at him): Until it's over.
NIO: Who decides when it's over?
REFEREE (beat, eyes narrowing): ...Next question.

[Jules laughs at something Kai says from two chairs away. WHISTLE. Second yellow. Kai's face drops.]
REFEREE: Second yellow. That's a red. You're broken up.
[Jules gasps. Kai stands up. Maya grabs Nio's arm and squeezes it like she's watching a penalty shootout.]
MAYA (urgent): Do something.

[Nio stands up and holds out his phone.]
NIO: Ref. I want a review.
[The referee stops. Considers it. Touches his ear like he's listening to someone. Nods.]

[The PXI album "roof 🌇" comes up big on the phone. The referee leans in, Maya leans in, the whole roof
crowds behind them like a match replay. Swipe: 6:12, Jules and Kai holding hands. Swipe: 6:31, sharing one
drink with two straws. Swipe: 6:40, asleep on each other's shoulder. Swipe: 6:55, Kai feeding Jules a chip.]
[The referee looks at the photos for a long time. He takes the whistle out of his mouth.]
REFEREE (quietly): ...This was never a break.
[He tears up both cards. Jules and Kai run at each other. The roof cheers. Maya cries into Nio's sleeve.]

[Later. Lights on. Everyone gone. The referee sits on the edge of the roof, alone, looking at the city. Nio
sits down next to him and offers him the chips.]
REFEREE (taking one, not looking at him): Fourteen years. Nobody's ever finished one.
[Hold. Room tone. A siren somewhere far off. He eats the chip.]
```

Laughs: the referee nobody looks at / "That's the break." / yellow card for the chips / "Until it's over."
"...Next question." / Maya's penalty-shootout grip / "Ref. I want a review." / the album as the replay /
"This was never a break."
Eerie: the referee alone at the end, and how long he has been doing this.
Caption: my friends went on a break and it came with a referee. Was it ever a break?

## 5. Good Game

Where: a game bar · Chore: lose to mango_dad again · Dodge: Nio turns up in person to win the way adults win
· The impossible thing: an eleven-year-old who is completely, eerily calm, and a bedtime that is a law of
nature · PXI: the tournament on the map, the photo for his mum · About 55 s

```
[INT. NIO'S APARTMENT, NIGHT. On the TV: DEFEAT. The winner: mango_dad. Nio's PXI map: "Arcade Night Finals,
tonight 8 pm." In the bracket: mango_dad.]
NIO (standing, grabbing his jacket): Online he's got good wifi. In person it's just hands.

[INT. GAME BAR. Neon. Nio walks in cracking his knuckles and sizes up every grown man in a dad shirt. He picks
the biggest one and stares him down. The man is holding a baby.]
HOST (on mic): Finals. Nio... and mango_dad.
[Across from Nio sits a BOY, eleven, green hoodie, juice box. Behind him, his MUM, reading a book.]
NIO: ...Hi.
MANGO_DAD: Hi.

[Round one. The boy wins one-handed, eating crackers with the other.]
[Nio leans across. Trash talk, in full, the way he would do it to Theo.]
NIO: Little man. Respectfully. Your bedtime's coming up and you're in your flop era.
[The boy looks at him, totally calm, and drinks his juice through the straw until it makes the sound.]
MANGO_DAD: You jump when you're scared.
[Round two. Every time Nio's character takes a hit, Nio jumps in his seat. The bar starts going "OH" on every
jump. He jumps. "OH." He jumps. "OH." The boy wins.]

[Nio puts the controller down. Breathes. Holds out his hand, meaning it.]
NIO: Good game.
MANGO_DAD (shaking it): Good game.
MUM (getting her phone out): Sorry. Would you take one of us?
[Nio gets low, finds the light, waits for the boy to stop blinking. PXI flash. The photo comes up warm and
lovely: the boy and his mum and a tiny trophy. The best photo anyone has taken in this bar.]
MUM: Oh. That's beautiful.

[The boy looks at his watch. 8:59. He stands up mid-sentence.]
MANGO_DAD: Bedtime.
[At 9:00 exactly, every screen in the bar goes dark at once. Silence. Neon hum. They walk out. The screens
come back on.]

[Nio, alone at the cabinet, picks up the controller and selects TRAINING MODE. Theo slides in beside him.]
THEO: Did you win?
NIO (eyes on the screen): He was eleven, bro.
```

Laughs: staring down the man holding a baby / the juice box reveal / "you're in your flop era" / the straw
sound / the bar going "OH" every time he jumps / the beautiful photo / every screen dying at 9:00 /
"He was eleven, bro."
Eerie: how calm the boy is, and the screens.
Caption: spent two years losing to mango_dad online. Finally met him. Who's the kid who always beats you?

## 6. Tiger

The founder's own story (R1) and already the strongest thing we have. It stays as written in `episodes.md`,
with two changes to bring it up to this standard: the three bathroom-door shots get the full set-piece
treatment (the music swells like a heist every time the door opens and cuts dead every time it shuts), and
the walk home is the eerie stretch: the street empty, the music in his earbuds small and far away, one shop
light buzzing, and Nio laughing to himself once.
