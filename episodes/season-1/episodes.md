## 1. The Move

Where: a club, Theo's birthday · Kind: a small thing balloons · Ego: Nio, drunk and sure he is the best
dancer in the room · Theory: "I have never regretted a single thing" · PXI: the album the next morning, then
the fourth wall · From life: the founder's drunk dancing and the contacts · About 40 s

```
[INT. CLUB, NIGHT. Bass, lights, a packed floor. NIO, three drinks in and glowing, is doing a dance that
should not work and does: a slow-motion lasso into a shopping-cart push into something like a man
reversing a car. The circle around him is loving it. THEO films him on his phone.]

THEO (shouting over the music): Eighty percent you regret this tomorrow.
NIO (mid-move, delighted): Theo. I have never regretted one thing in my life.

[He spins. His head snaps. Freeze on his face. Cut to his point of view: the club is a smear of colour.]

NIO: ...Okay. One thing.

[He drops into a crouch and starts sweeping the floor with both hands, slow circles, head low, squinting.]

[A GIRL next to him watches, decides this is a move, and crouches beside him, sweeping in time.]
[Her two friends copy her.]
[The DJ sees it, grins, and drops the beat down to a slow heavy thud. Sweep. Sweep.]
[Wide: the whole floor is crouched and sweeping in unison. Two hundred people. The lights go low and green.]

[Nio, blind, sees blurry shapes all around him at floor level.]
NIO (moved, quietly): You guys are helping.
GIRL (sweeping, thrilled): What's it called?
NIO (squinting at the floor, honestly): I lost it.
GIRL (standing, to the whole club): IT'S CALLED "I LOST IT"!
CLUB: I LOST IT!

[MAYA crouches next to him, plucks something off his shoulder, and puts it on his fingertip.]
MAYA (sincere, gentle): It was on you.

[He puts it in. The world snaps into focus: two hundred people in a crouch, sweeping the floor, chanting.
Hold on his face.]

NIO: ...I'm not gonna stop them.

[He gets back down and sweeps with everyone. The DJ drops the beat back in. The whole floor rises at once.]

[CUT TO: NIO'S APARTMENT, MORNING. Clean. Nio on the couch, sunglasses on indoors, phone up. The PXI shared
album "theo's 22nd 🪩" is open. At the top, Theo's flash photo: the whole club crouched, and in the middle,
Nio, squinting at the floor like a man reading a contract.]

NIO (to us, deadly serious): That's a dance now.
NIO (holding up the phone): Theo took it. It's in my album. This my app. Go download PXI.
```

Laughs: the drop from "never regretted one thing" to "...okay, one thing" / the whole club sweeping /
"IT'S CALLED 'I LOST IT'" / the sharp picture of two hundred people crouching / the squint in the photo.
Caption: when you lose a contact at the club and accidentally start a dance. Did you do the move? 🪩

## 2. Password

Where: a speakeasy behind a laundromat · Kind: the lead's theory against a closed door · Ego: Nio's
certainty that a password is a vibe · Theory: "It's a laundromat. It's a fluffy thing." · PXI: the event page,
in Maya's hand · About 40 s

```
[EXT. LAUNDROMAT, NIGHT. Rows of dryers turning. At the back, a narrow door with a slot. NIO, MAYA and SAMI,
dressed up. Nio knocks. The slot slides open: two eyes.]

DOORMAN: Password.
NIO (instantly, smooth): Fluffy.
[Slot shuts.]

MAYA: Did they send a password?
NIO: Maya, it's a laundromat. It's a fluffy thing. It's vibes. Watch.
[Knock. Slot.]
NIO: Tumble.
[Shut. Knock. Slot.]
NIO: Lint.
[Shut. Knock. Slot. The eyes are tired now.]
NIO (gently, like a doctor): Delicates.
[A long look. Shut.]

[The queue behind them has grown, and the queue has opinions.]
GUY IN QUEUE: Try "spin cycle."
GIRL IN QUEUE: It's "softener." It's obviously "softener."
SAMI (stepping up, formal): I would like to try "starch." Thank you for asking.
[Slot. Shut. Without a word.]

[Nio, now fully in a bit, puts on a tired lawyer's voice and knocks.]
NIO: Sir. I represent every sock that ever went missing in this building.
[The eyes crinkle. The slot stays open one second longer than it has to. Then shuts.]
NIO (to the queue, thrilled): He liked that one.

[IMANE walks past the whole queue, eyes on her phone, and knocks.]
DOORMAN: Password.
IMANE (not looking up): No.
[The door swings open. She goes in. It shuts.]

[Silence. The whole queue turns and looks at Nio.]
NIO (beat, the realist): ...It's on the event page.
MAYA (already holding up her phone: the PXI event page, "Password: No"): It's on the event page.
NIO (humble): Yeah. That was me.

[He knocks. Slot.]
NIO: No.
DOORMAN (looking right at him): No.
[Shut.]

[Nio looks at the door for a long beat, and nods slowly, with respect.]
NIO: ...That's fair.
```

Laughs: "Delicates", said gently / the queue joining in / the sock lawyer and "he liked that one" / Imane's
"No" / the doorman's "No" back / "That's fair".
Caption: he tried 11 passwords. It was on the event page. What would you have said? 🧺

## 3. On a Break

Where: a rooftop at golden hour, string lights, folding chairs · Kind: a small thing balloons · Ego: Maya's
romance, and nobody believing Nio is really asking · Theory (Maya's): a break is the most romantic thing a
couple can do · PXI: the rooftop album · About 45 s

```
[EXT. ROOFTOP, GOLDEN HOUR. Friends on folding chairs. Across from Nio and Maya, a couple: JULES and KAI,
sitting together, sharing one blanket.]

MAYA (leaning in, a whisper that is not a whisper): They're on a break.
NIO (looking at them, under one blanket): From what?
MAYA: From each other.
NIO: So they broke up.
MAYA: No. A break.
NIO: Who's timing it?
MAYA: Stop.
NIO: I'm asking.
[She checks his face. He is asking.]
MAYA: Oh my god. You're actually asking.

THEO (from the next chair): Seventy percent they're back by Friday.
NIO (to Jules, perfectly polite): Sorry. Is it a week, or is it like a vibe?
JULES: It's... we didn't say.
KAI: I thought it was a week.
JULES: You said "a bit."
KAI: A bit is a week.
JULES: A bit is a bit, Kai.
NIO (honestly): Can you see other people on it?
[Everyone on the roof goes quiet.]
JULES and KAI (to each other, at once): Are you seeing other people?

[It spreads down the row of chairs. Two people who are not even dating start asking each other what their
rules are. Theo is doing sums on a napkin.]

MAYA (standing, taking charge, phone out): Okay. Okay. Everybody look.
[She opens the PXI album "roof 🌇". Scrolls. Every single photo from tonight: Jules and Kai holding hands.
Sharing a drink. Asleep on each other's shoulder at 6:40. Kai feeding Jules a chip.]

[Jules and Kai look at the photos. Then at each other. Their hands find each other under the blanket.]
KAI (quietly): ...We're bad at breaks.

[Maya bursts into tears of joy and grabs Nio's arm.]
MAYA: That is the most romantic thing I've ever seen.
NIO (watching the couple, still honestly confused): So who was timing it?
```

Laughs: "Who's timing it?" / "You're actually asking" / "A bit is a bit, Kai" / the whole roof asking each
other / the album / Maya's tears against Nio's last question.
Caption: he asked one honest question and the whole roof had to define the relationship. Is a break a week or
a vibe?

## 4. Good Game

Where: a game bar · Kind: a side character gets the reveal · Ego: Nio's, then put down quietly · Theory:
"Online he's got good wifi. In person it's just hands." · PXI: the tournament on the map · About 40 s

```
[INT. NIO'S APARTMENT, NIGHT. Nio on the floor, controller in hand. On the TV: DEFEAT. Again. The winner's
name: mango_dad. On his phone, the PXI map: "Arcade Night: Finals. Tonight. 8 pm." In the bracket: mango_dad.]

NIO (quietly, to the screen, standing up): Online, he's got good wifi. In person it's just hands.

[INT. GAME BAR. Neon, cabinets, a small crowd. Nio walks in, cracking his knuckles, scanning for a grown man
in a dad shirt. He finds three. He sizes each one up. None of them are it.]
HOST (on a mic): Finals! Nio... and mango_dad!

[Across from Nio sits a BOY, about eleven, in a green hoodie, with a juice box. Behind him, his MUM, with a
tote bag and a book.]

[Nio looks at the boy. At the mum. At the boy.]
NIO: ...Hi.
MANGO_DAD: Hi.

[The match. Nio is good: fast, sweating, leaning into it. The boy plays one-handed while eating crackers.
The boy wins.]

[Hold on Nio. Then he puts the controller down, stands, and holds out his hand.]
NIO (humble, meaning it): Good game.
MANGO_DAD (shaking it): You jump when you're scared.
NIO (beat): ...I do do that.

MUM (getting her phone out): Sorry, could you take one of us?
[Nio takes her phone, gets low, finds the light, waits for the boy to stop blinking. Flash. The PXI photo
comes up: the boy and his mum with the little trophy, warm and lovely, the best photo anyone in this bar
has taken tonight.]
MUM: Oh, that's beautiful.

[MANGO_DAD looks at his watch.]
MANGO_DAD: Bedtime.
[They leave mid-sentence. The crowd goes with them.]

[Nio, alone at the cabinet. He sits down, picks up the controller, and selects TRAINING MODE.]
```

Laughs: the three grown men he sizes up / the reveal with the juice box / crackers one-handed / "You jump
when you're scared" / "I do do that" / "Bedtime".
Caption: spent two years trying to beat mango_dad. Met him tonight. Who's the kid who always beats you?

## 5. Potential

Where: campus, the gym, the corner shop, a park · Kind: authority, as a chase · Ego: Nio's dread of being
given advice · Theory: "If she can't find me, she can't give me the talk." · PXI: the map, to get as far away
as possible · About 45 s

```
[INT. CORRIDOR, DAY. NIO, earbuds in, walking out of class. At the far end of the corridor: DR. OKAFOR,
holding a folder.]
DR. OKAFOR: Nio. A word.

[Nio takes one earbud out. Looks at her. Puts it back in. Turns smoothly into the nearest door.]

[INT. POTTERY CLASS. Ten students at wheels. Nio sits down at a free wheel and starts throwing a pot like
he has done it for years. It is a good pot. In the door's window: Dr. Okafor, waiting.]

[INT. GYM. Nio on a bike, earbuds in, pedalling hard. On the bike next to him: Dr. Okafor, pedalling
calmly, folder on the handlebars.]
DR. OKAFOR: You know, at your age...
[Nio is off the bike and gone.]

[INT. CORNER SHOP, NIGHT. Nio, breathing hard, behind the shelves. Dr. Okafor at the counter, buying milk.
She doesn't turn around.]
DR. OKAFOR: Thursday's fine too.

[EXT. STREET. Nio, walking fast, opens the PXI map, pinches out as far as it goes, and taps the event
furthest from campus: "Chess in the Park."]

[EXT. PARK, DUSK. Long tables of chess boards under lights. Nio sits at a free board, relieved, and puts his
earbuds back in. His opponent turns around. It is Dr. Okafor. She moves a pawn.]

[Nio looks at the board for a long time. Takes both earbuds out.]
NIO (humble): Okay. What's the word.
DR. OKAFOR (delighted, opening the folder): Your lab report. You spelled your own name wrong.
[Beat.]
NIO: ...On purpose.
DR. OKAFOR: Twice.
[She takes his knight.]
```

Laughs: the earbud out and back in / the good pot / the bike / "Thursday's fine too" / the chess reveal /
"You spelled your own name wrong" / "Twice".
Caption: ran from my professor all day. She just wanted to tell me this. Who are you avoiding right now?

## 6. Tiger (LONG, with a short cut)

From the founder's story R1, names and details changed. Where: a house party, the bathroom, the curb, a car,
the walk home, bed · Kind: the quest · Ego: Sami's dignity · Theory (Sami's): if nobody saw it, it didn't
happen · PXI: the party album the next morning · Long cut about 3 minutes, short cut about 45 s

**The beats (long cut).**

1. **The party** (someone else's house, packed). Nio, drinking, no contacts in tonight, which we see when he
   squints at a fridge magnet from two inches away. Sami, two drinks in, is gone: leaning at an angle that
   should not be possible, insisting he is "completely fine, thank you for asking".
2. **The carry.** Nio gets Sami's arm over his shoulder and walks him to the bathroom, doing a commentary in
   a nature-documentary voice the whole way.
3. **The door.** A minute later a stranger runs up: "Yo. Your friend is not good in there." Nio knocks, opens
   the door: Sami is standing perfectly upright at the sink, washing his hands, composed as a butler. He nods.
   Nio, baffled, steps in and closes the door. Sami folds instantly to the floor. Nio opens the door a crack
   to look out: Sami is upright again. Closes it: floor. **Three shots, no words.** Nio cannot breathe for
   laughing.
4. **Sleeves up.** The laugh stops in one frame. Nio cleans up, quickly and quietly, and gets Sami to his feet.
5. **The ride.** On the curb, Nio squints at his phone, sees "get it faster" and taps it. (It was a shared
   ride.)
6. **The pants.** He sits Sami down, steps back, and sees dark spots all over Sami's trousers. "NO. Don't sit
   down. Bro. You got it all over—" He leans in until his nose nearly touches them. They are tiger print.
   Sami, with great dignity: "They're tiger."
7. **The car.** The car arrives. The back seat already holds three passengers speaking a language Nio does
   not know, completely calm, making room for a man being loaded in sideways. Nio: "Why are there people in
   here?" Driver: "You booked shared." Nio, to Sami: "Do you have your phone?" Sami: "No." Nio: "Can you do
   this?" Sami: "Get away from me." Door shuts. Nio, to the driver, through the window, hands together:
   "Please. Please get him home. He's very important to me."
8. **The walk.** A friend drops Nio off. He doesn't go in. He walks. Both earbuds in, the street empty, the
   music small. He laughs once, to himself, about the sink.
9. **5:04 a.m.** Bed. His phone lights up: a message from Sami. "hmoe sfae bor" and a thumbs up. Nio laughs
   until he is crying into the pillow. "This guy can't be real."
10. **The morning.** The party's shared album lands on PXI. Sami is upright and dignified in every single
    photo, holding a drink he never drank. In the last one, behind him, a blur of Nio carrying him.

**The short cut (about 45 s):** 1 (one shot), 3 (the door, all three shots), 6 (the pants), 7 (the car, from
"Why are there people" to "Get away from me"), 9 (the message and the laugh). The album is in the long cut
only.

Caption: carried my friend home and almost cried over his tiger pants. Who's the lightweight in your group?
