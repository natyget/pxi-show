# The series: Nio's world

The founder, Nov 24: "I really want a series where we follow a interesting character that we just want to follow
because it's funny stories that he encounters, you know, like the amazing world of Gumball. And you would just
follow Gumball. But imagine Gumball created Pixie and he's using it, and he showcases it. Like, within the show,
you see him using the app and all that." And: "He shouldn't be always talking to us."

That is the idea only: a character people follow for his stories. Nothing of that show's cast, look or jokes.

## What it is

Nio built PXI, and he is not good at the thing his app is for. His plans go wrong, his friends are no help, and
he stays sure of himself the whole way through. An episode is one small funny story from his life, whole on its
own, 30 to 45 seconds. Somewhere in it he uses the app the way a person would, and the real screen does one
thing. Nobody explains the app. People come back for him.

## Who is in it

Nio and his friends from the rig: Maya, Sami, Theo, Imane (`look/rig.md`). Here they have names, because the
viewer meets them. Who each one is comes from what they do across episodes, never from a description.
They talk in their own voices with their mouths moving (`look/rig.md`, "Talking"). A narrator is rare and quick.
Nio talks to us only now and then.

## The shape of an episode

Learned from where people left "idk to omw" (the founder read its first hours): about one in five swiped away
between second 3 and second 4. The hook ended at 3.2 s; then a second opening began ("Friday, nine P.M.
Everyone's waiting for someone else to pick."), said slowly over Nio sitting on his bed for five seconds. The
first joke came at second 26. The founder: "it's taking too long. It's not going to the point. It's not fast
enough, and it's not entertaining enough either ... not punchy enough. It's not also funny."

- **Second 0 to 2:** already in the middle of the mess, on the funniest or strangest picture, with a line said.
- **By second 3** we know what he wants. No second opening: no day, no time, no place read out after the hook.
- **Something funny at least every six seconds,** the first before second 5. Funny is a picture or a line that
  surprises. A caption that explains is not a joke.
- **No picture holds longer than about two and a half seconds in the first ten.**
- **The app is his move,** on screen by second ten at the latest, a few seconds at a time, whole and centred
  (`look/look.md`).
- **The last picture is the punchline.** The logo is the last second and a half, never five.

## How we learn

After each episode: the second, the picture and the word where people left, and where they stayed or watched
again. The apps' own retention graphs show it (the founder sends a screenshot or says it); Metricool gives the
averages. One line per episode in the test log in `STATE.md`. This page changes when the numbers say so.
