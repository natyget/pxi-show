# Don't

Mistakes the old engine made again and again. Read it every session. `studio/check.py` scans scripts and captions
for the phrases in section 1. This list does not grow with every note: a new note means the maker was not thinking
as the viewer (`studio/viewer.md`), so fix that, and fix the cause in the tool or the template.

## 1. Words (banned in scripts, captions and on-screen text)

- "the morning after", "the next morning": the feature is the scrapbook or the album.
- Any ticket security claim ("can't be screenshotted", "can't be faked"). Tickets go in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet; say no more.
- "The ticket, the camera, the morning after": a fake tagline from an outside handoff.
- Fee talk: "cheaper than", fee comparisons, "Eventbrite raised fees".
- "welcome to", "check out PXI", "find us on the App Store" (show the badges instead), "like and follow", "comment below", "tag a friend", "tag the one who".
- "Part 1", "Part 2": every release is whole.
- Stock lines that kept coming back: "the group album", "lost and found", "the best night of", "thank you for asking", "a clock with no hands", "contacts back in", "this can't be real", "pics or it didn't happen", "no pics of me", "who is this", "yeah what's up", "I'm just tired", "they left".
- Retired jokes: the elbow, "evidence" or "proof" talk, "the actual guy".
- The "you're the X guy" / "I'm not being the X guy" construction.
- Filler and staged run-ups: "literally", "vibes", "chaos", "honestly", "here's the thing", "I mean", "you know", "absolutely".
- Anyone saying the joke or the moral out loud, or explaining what the viewer just saw.
- Made-up "cool" names for people, places or events: Late Checkout, Gala Noir, Velvet Hour, Kofi, Zoe, Tess, Nia, Ama, Dev, Beanie. Use plain names: Sarah, Emily, Mike, Jessica, Lauren, Chris, Ashley.
- Nio never says "hold up", "ight" or a drawn-out "nahhh".

## 2. Writing

- Lines of "3 to 7 flat words". Nio talks like a quick, funny 21-year-old.
- Forwarding the founder's message to a worker as it is. Turn it into a short brief first.
- A plot that is a product placement. PXI shows up the way a person really uses it, tonight.
- Carrying a joke, name or reference from an old script into a new one.

## 3. Look and acting

- A character looking into the lens. In the old engine this was a code default (the face turned to the camera, and
  the `smug` and `charm` faces lifted one brow with the pupils on us). Eyes go to the person spoken to or the thing
  in the shot. A look to camera only when the script asks for one.
- Faces with no mouth, blank eyes, eyes all glancing the same way, mouths that only flap up and down.
- Everyone standing in a row; a crowd all staring at Nio; a shot where nothing happens; a still image with a slow zoom.
- A line said by someone off screen or by a back filling the frame, so the viewer cannot tell who talks.
- Something happening with no cause on screen.
- Hands holding a phone that are not the character's own hands.
- Party scenes in Nio's bedroom. Event covers on app screens that are Nio's room.
- The same few poses reused in every post.

## 4. Format and posting

- Text behind the Dynamic Island or the app's buttons (see the safe zones in `method.md`).
- A 4:5 slide posted as a 9:16 story. Each placement gets its own render.
- A reel with no sound, a homemade "beat", or AI music. A reel hidden from the grid by accident.
- Posting a draft, or leaving a scheduled post as a draft by accident. Read the schedule back after scheduling.
- Promising a song before testing that the account can use it.
- Pills, chips, tag rows, eyebrow labels (a small line above a heading), badges under a heading, giant numerals, neon or purple gradient backgrounds, "PXI." with a dot.
- A carousel slide reused as a story.
